## What is this?

For a plain-language take on the vision, see: https://youtu.be/xfiZfF2Y6K8?si=XogD75Ow--5zG5Z9

**The Infinite Runway** is the public, **ongoing** work to build a sustainable, creator-owned life and shared infrastructure — the **continuation of The 60 Day Runway** and the **origin story of The ARX Foundation**. There is **no** fixed end date: the “runway” here means sustained lift (resources, knowledge, and community) for as long as it takes. It is still a test of self-rescue, autonomy, and creative momentum, documented in the open.

This is not just a hustle. The goal remains to **challenge the myth of merit**, surface the **hidden architecture of economic traps**, and **ship what we learn** so others in survival loops are less alone.

## The mission

Reform the education and knowledge landscape we inherit. We are in a time of misinformation, fear, and turmoil; now is as good a time as any to make change, so that is what we are doing.

Specifically: “Make Academia Great Again” by processing and centralizing knowledge on a **publicly owned** platform ([DO2E // SCOOL // The Creostic Academia](6%20-%20Projects/_Requests%20for%20Support/Building%20a%20Better%20Future%20Through%20Business%20and%20AI%20Application.md)) and **decentralizing the tools** to access and use knowledge. The end goal is a **marketplace of intellectual acceleration** — methods and strategies that respect that **people can think for themselves** and that that should be a defensible, secure idea.

## Why?

Generational motivations: **because we have been trained to think we cannot** — cannot own, cannot escape, cannot think for ourselves, cannot make the world better.

General team motivations: **because we can.**

Landon’s motivations: graduated with honors and still could not afford rent. My wife deserves a degree without our lives collapsing. I want to build something that helps others do the same.

**Add your motivations here if you help out.**

We believe in making it visible — in **livestreaming** the climb, the mistakes, the breakthroughs, and the burnout. The current market often lacks **transparency**. Open source, here, does not mean “community-washed”; it means **as much transparency and accountability** as the work allows.
