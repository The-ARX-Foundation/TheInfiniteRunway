## Guiding Principles

1. **Never reveal stealth project names externally.** Use broad categories only.
2. **Public evidence trail is the strongest weapon.** Timestamps, commits, licenses.
3. **Tone over legalese.** Peter is not an adversary. Lisa is the gatekeeper on the other side.
4. **A raise doesn't replace written IP protection.** More money is nice but doesn't protect nonprofit work.
5. **Retroactivity is clarification, not renegotiation.** "This is what we've always understood."
6. **ARX Foundation is nonprofit. Always. Non-negotiable.** Commercial work goes through [[Hopefully Abysmal]] LLC.
