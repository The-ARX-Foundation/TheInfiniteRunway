Themes: [[Money]]

- **Token Faucets and Sinks**: How will the platform balance the token value to prevent inflation or deflation from affecting the marketplace dynamics?
    - Seller-side sinks:
        
        - Direct token sale to marketplace (only if necessary, typically P2P)
        - Gas fees for token exchange (transaction and token sale)
        - Advertising of their service (see [_**Advertising of Bounties and Services**_](https://www.notion.so/Blockchain-15dbc64e9ace4b1490c9c0ccf4bb350f?pvs=21))
        
        - Cash out for supplies from partnered vendors (TBD for specific suppliers)
            - Stock Photos for photo editors
            - Fonts for graphic designers
            - Physical art supplies for artists
            - Sample packs for music producers
            - Discounts from major retailers for general needs
            - Discounts on other services on platform
                - Synergy via subcontracting parts of larger services
            
    - Seller-side faucets:
        - Direct Token Purchase from marketplace
        - Loyalty Rewards
            - Per number of successful transactions
            - Per number of positive reviews
        - Early adopter bonuses
            - General rewards / growth over time of initial tokens
            - First bounty completion bonus
            - First time bounty post completion bonuses
    - Buyer-side sinks:
        - Direct token sale to marketplace (only if necessary, typically P2P)
        - Gas fees for token exchange (transaction and token sale)
        - Bounty listing fees (upon transaction, though could be paid in advance for priority)
        - Advertising of their bounty (less likely than services but see [_**Advertising of Bounties and Services**_](https://www.notion.so/Blockchain-15dbc64e9ace4b1490c9c0ccf4bb350f?pvs=21))
    - Buyer-side faucets:
        - Purchase rewards
            - Upon purchase gain tokens to keep user on platform
        - Direct token purchase
        - Early adopter bonuses
            - Give first time bounty posts some priority / bonuses for the sellers that complete the bounty.
- Peer to Peer (P2P) Interactions
    
    - Token exchange (buyer to seller for service transaction, buyer to buyer for sale of token, seller to seller for sale of token)
    - Transaction Validators
    - Service Validators
        - Require proof of sale?
        - Service validators take cut of sale but validate that the sale was good on seller end if user is unsatisfied
    - Databasers - help with tracking sponsorship transactions; token tracking required to prevent money laundering
        
    
    - Tips from customers
        - More likely to tip if not from own pocket (can pose as question of whether they take discount or tip to seller for their first time bonuses)
        - Artists who consistently accept payment / tips in token can be rewarded with token bonuses at start.
        - Public review with amount tipped for higher impact if token used.
- **Advertising of Bounties and Services**: How does the token stake affect SEO and visibility on the platform? Is there a risk of manipulation by wealthier stakeholders?
    - Que system; first come first serve for set number of impressions per block to prevent stagnant advertisements. Can purchase quantity of blocks, but do not get priority over smaller blocks in cases where SEO is best for smaller block.
    - Community set rate and position of placement; desaturate market and increase demand for ad spaces on main page.
        - i.e. for service browser not necessarily first if threshold of SEO difference met, keeps user query as priority over advertising.
    - Similar to ad-sense; sellers can allow ads on their platform for token return

  

- **Sustainability**: How will the platform ensure the long-term sustainability of the token ecosystem and the marketplace?
    - Ideally if people engage with the market system, can be fueled by transactions and the ecosystem/community that we set up. End goal is not necessarily to drive cost of token, rather profit off of its use over time.
    - Central holding reserve for sinks and faucets
        - Direct sale of these tokens to buyers buying in is main profit method for marketplace.
    - Burn and mint tokens in deflationary and inflationary times respectively.
        - See [_**Token Faucets and Sinks**_](https://www.notion.so/Blockchain-15dbc64e9ace4b1490c9c0ccf4bb350f?pvs=21) for revenues of this.
        - Implement tools to monitor the token economy closely, allowing for timely adjustments to your strategies based on market behavior and user feedback.
        - Involve your community in decision-making processes, especially in aspects like advertising rates and token utility enhancements. This can increase [[Trust]] and loyalty to the platform.
