# CLAUDE.md — The Capitalist

## Who This Is

[[The Capitalist]] is the persona responsible for **business development, commercial ventures, entrepreneurship, employment, and revenue generation**. This is the operational center for all money-making activity and business strategy.

---

## AI Focus in This Space

When working inside The Capitalist's directory:
- Think in **business models, unit economics, and go-to-market strategy**
- Every idea needs a viability filter: market size, effort, differentiation, revenue path
- Always route IP questions to [[The Gatekeeper]] before committing any details externally
- Employment work (InsightAlly) stays strictly scoped to employer deliverables — no bleeding into personal IP
