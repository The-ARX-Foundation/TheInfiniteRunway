# SIDE TOOLING & INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY BOUNDARY AGREEMENT

*(Addendum and Clarification to Independent Contractor and/or Employment Agreements)*

This Side Tooling & Intellectual Property Boundary Agreement (“**Boundary Agreement**”) is entered into by and between:

- **Company:** Insight-Ally, LLC  
- **Developer:** [[Landon Dahle]]  
- **Developer Entity:** [[Hopefully Abysmal]] LLC  
- **Effective Date:** [DATE]

---

## 1. Purpose and Retroactive Clarification

The parties enter into this Boundary Agreement to **clarify and confirm** the ownership boundaries between:

(a) Company-owned deliverables created for Company; and  
(b) Side Tooling and Background IP owned by **[[Hopefully Abysmal]] LLC**.

**For the avoidance of doubt**, this Agreement applies retroactively to Developer’s start date with Company (October 13, 2025) and is intended to **clarify, not modify**, the parties’ original intent regarding ownership of generalized development tooling, workflow systems, and external automation infrastructure.

This Agreement further acknowledges the existence of **publicly documented Background IP** owned by [[Hopefully Abysmal]] LLC, including but not limited to materials published under the **ARX Foundation Public Pact (AFPP)** at:

> https://github.com/Hopefully-Abysmal/The60DayRunway

Such publication evidences independent origination and intent to retain ownership, subject to the terms of this Agreement.

---

## 2. Definitions

### A. Company Platform
The Company’s proprietary software products, low-code IDE(s), runtime(s), internal systems, repositories, customer instances, and related proprietary infrastructure.

---

### B. Services
The services Developer was engaged to perform for Company as defined in **Exhibit A of the applicable Contractor or Employment Agreement**, including data analysis, workflow configuration, reporting, and platform implementation expressly requested by Company.

---

### C. Company Deliverables
Any work product created by Developer that:

1. is specifically requested or assigned by Company;  
2. is created primarily to satisfy Company customer or internal requirements; and  
3. is created within the scope of the Services and intended to be deployed within or executed by the Company Platform.

**Company Deliverables expressly exclude** general-purpose tools, translators, generators, linters, converters, workflow engines, productivity systems, or automation frameworks that merely assist in the creation of Company Deliverables and are not themselves deployed as part of the Company Platform.

---

### D. Side Tooling
Developer-created tools, systems, and methods that are:

1. **General-purpose** in nature;  
2. **Portable** across employers, clients, or projects; and  
3. **Not specific** to Company customers, Company data, or Company proprietary infrastructure.

Including, without limitation:

- IDE extensions and editor plugins  
- Workflow translators, generators, and migration tools  
- Automation engines and orchestration frameworks  
- OS-level workspace and environment systems  
- Cross-platform workflow replication tooling  
- Educational materials and public content  

Incidental use of Side Tooling in Company work does not transfer ownership.

---

### E. Background IP
All Side Tooling or other intellectual property owned by **[[Hopefully Abysmal]] LLC** that:

- was conceived, authored, or reduced to practice **prior to Developer’s start date**, **and/or**
- has been publicly released, timestamped, or licensed (including repositories, vaults, documentation, and associated tooling),

together with independent improvements, refinements, and derivative systems that do **not** embed Company Confidential Information or Company Deliverables.

---

### F. Company Confidential Information
Non-public information belonging to Company or its customers, including trade secrets, customer data, internal roadmaps, pricing, and security information.

---

## 3. Ownership and Assignment

### 3.1 Company Ownership
Company owns all Company Deliverables.

---

### 3.2 Express Exclusion and Ownership Vesting
Notwithstanding any provision in:

- the Independent Contractor Agreement,  
- any Offer Letter, or  
- any Proprietary Information and Inventions Agreement,

**Company acknowledges and agrees** that all right, title, and interest in Side Tooling and Background IP is and shall remain vested exclusively in **Hopefully Abysmal LLC**, except as expressly licensed under this Agreement.

---

### 3.3 No Transfer by Tool Use
Use of Side Tooling:

- on Company-provided devices,  
- during Company working hours, or  
- in support of Company work,

does **not** constitute assignment, work-for-hire, or implied license beyond Section 4.

The existence of publicly available versions, prior commits, or licensed releases of Side Tooling shall constitute **rebuttable evidence** that such tooling is not a Company Deliverable.

---

### 3.4 Platform Artifacts vs. External Tools
- Artifacts instantiated **inside** the Company Platform → Company Deliverables  
- Tools operating **outside** the Company Platform → Side Tooling  

This classification applies even where external tools generate, validate, migrate, or accelerate Company Platform artifacts.

---

## 4. License to Use Side Tooling (Internal Use Only)

If Developer or Hopefully Abysmal LLC affirmatively provides Side Tooling for Company use, Hopefully Abysmal LLC grants Company a **non-exclusive, non-transferable, non-sublicensable, internal-use-only license** for the duration of Developer’s engagement and **[X] months thereafter**.

This license does **not** permit reverse engineering, replication, sublicensing, or commercialization of the Side Tooling.

---

## 5. Clean-Room & Resource Use Clarification

Use of Company-provided devices (including operating systems required for communications tooling) does not collapse IP boundaries or convert Side Tooling or Background IP owned by Hopefully Abysmal LLC into Company Deliverables.

---

## 6. Conflict Resolution Window

Company must dispute classification of any tooling or IP within **30 days** of written notice.  
Failure to do so constitutes [[Acceptance]] of the classification.

---

## 7. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of **Texas**, without regard to conflict-of-law principles.

---

## 8. Entire Agreement; Supersession

This Boundary Agreement supersedes inconsistent IP provisions **solely with respect to Side Tooling and Background IP** and does not otherwise alter Company ownership of Company Deliverables or Company Platform IP.

Nothing in this Agreement obligates either party to enter into a joint venture, partnership, or equity arrangement; any such relationship must be documented in a separate written agreement.
