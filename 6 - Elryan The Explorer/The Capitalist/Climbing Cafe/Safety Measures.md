Safety should be a top priority in your climbing gym. Implement proper safety protocols, such as mandatory safety orientations, trained staff members, regular inspections of equipment, and adequate crash pads or mats to cushion falls. Install safety systems like auto-belay devices or harness systems to support climbers.

  

Belay system will be top rope via pipes akin to Canyons Climbing Gym. Build the walls such that there are doorways to a stairway underneath the pipe such that the climber can just climb up and clip into guard rail by side to ensure safety. Walk up some stairs to platform that leads to the cafe. To get down, auto belay [[Trust]] fall or elevator.

  

CWA Certification Notes:

- A written emergency action plan (EAP)
- Staff are trained how to implement the EAP
- EAP can cover the following potential emergencies - first aid,  
    environmental spills and hazards, fire, natural disasters, evacuation,  
    active shooter, potentially rescues from height
