Outdoor climbers are enthusiasts who prefer climbing on natural rock formations. They seek opportunities to explore and climb in outdoor settings, such as mountains, crags, and cliffs. They may be interested in climbing trips, guided outdoor experiences, and access to outdoor climbing destinations.

- Organize outdoor climbing trips and expeditions to popular climbing destinations.
- Provide resources and information on nearby outdoor climbing areas, including guidebooks and route information.
- Collaborate with local outdoor adventure clubs or guides to offer guided outdoor climbing experiences.
- Host workshops on outdoor climbing skills, such as anchor building or multi-pitch techniques.
