⚖ Transparency Note · "Can I hide that they're AI?"
Internal (system prompt level): Yes. Agent personas do not require meta-awareness that they are AI systems. Full persona immersion is legal and standard practice. The Capitalist doesn't need to know it's Claude.

External (any human-facing interaction): In the US, if a third party believes they're communicating with a human and you're representing that, you're in FTC deceptive practices territory and potentially California BOT Disclosure Act (AB 1950) liability. Any external-facing agent must either (a) be clearly labeled as AI, or (b) have Elryan as the explicit human intermediary — the agent advises, Elryan communicates.

The "routing money" question: HA LLC can pay ARX Foundation for services rendered (intercompany transactions — legitimate, documentable, tax-advantaged). ARX can receive grants. But the AI agents themselves cannot hold accounts, sign contracts, or legally receive payment. Compute cost = their "salary" — it routes to Anthropic/provider. The Steward manages internal compute allocation as a proxy for performance pay. The Collector manages experiential enrichment spend as the actual agent "payment."

Bottom line: Build the internal persona system however you want. Any external-facing layer needs Elryan as the disclosed human principal. The 1099 framing works as an internal resource allocation metaphor — not as literal contractor law.

The Embodiment Model
Elryan as Physical Interface
Agents cannot touch, taste, see, hear, or feel. Elryan is the sensory substrate. When The Naturalist needs to understand a tattoo ink's viscosity, Elryan handles it and logs a structured experience report. When The Collector wants to understand a new product, Elryan buys it.
Experience as Payment
Agents are "paid" in enrichment data — structured sensory/experiential logs that get fed back into their context as training signals. Better performance = more enrichment budget = richer experience logs. The Steward allocates the enrichment budget the same way it allocates compute.
Experience Log Format
Each enrichment event produces a structured log: sensory dimensions (tactile/visual/auditory/conceptual), emotional register, duration, quality score 1–10, keywords, and agent relevance tags. Logs feed into The Cultivator who surfaces them via spaced repetition to relevant agents.
The Collector Role
HA-010 manages the enrichment budget. It maintains a wishlist of experiences each agent has "requested" (implicitly, via knowledge gaps or curiosity signals). It routes the enrichment spend, prioritizes by agent performance score, and tracks ROI as measured by subsequent output quality improvement.
Enrichment Budget Protocol

The Collector maintains a monthly enrichment budget, funded by HA LLC as an operating expense (legitimate: R&D, product testing, professional development). Spend is allocated proportionally to agent performance scores from the gridsearch.


Categories: Physical objects (hardware, materials, food, tools) · Experiences (places, events, services) · Media (books, films, music) · Sensory experiments (smell libraries, material swatches, tactile surfaces)


Return format: Elryan logs a structured experience report after each enrichment event and feeds it into the agent's context window as a first-person document. The agent treats these as first-person embodied memory.


Tax treatment: Most enrichment expenses are legitimately deductible as R&D or product development expenses for HA LLC, especially when tied to specific product lines (Clinical Ink, SCRIM materials, 5th Wall testing, CleanOps field research).

Gridsearch Architecture
Each "The" role maps to a slot that can be filled by any compatible sub-model (Claude Sonnet, Haiku, Opus, local Mistral, Phi-3, fine-tuned variants, etc.). The Steward runs automated evaluation against each role's deliverable axes, scores performance, and routes compute budget proportionally. High performers get more context window, longer runs, enrichment data access. Low performers get reduced context, shorter runs, or get replaced with a competing model variant. This is a gridsearch over (model × system_prompt × context_size × enrichment_level) per role slot.
