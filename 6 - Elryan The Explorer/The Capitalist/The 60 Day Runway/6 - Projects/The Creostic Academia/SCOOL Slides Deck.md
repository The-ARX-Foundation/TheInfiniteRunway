## 🧠 SCOOL 
The Scalable Community for Open Online Learning

(The Learning System for a Post-AI World)

---

## Why SCOOL?

Education is slow, expensive, and disconnected.  
Worse — it treats every learner the same.

The system lacks a map of your mind.  
So it teaches blindly — and hopes you keep up.

---
## Imagine This

**LinkedIn Learning meets SecondMe**  
— professional skill-building, fused with user-owned digital identity.

An AI that knows what _you_ know.  
And more importantly what you _don’t_ — yet.

---
## What We're Building

Our **aptitude engine** maps your knowledge  
to give you only the most relevant material:  

→ No repetition  
→ No irrelevant fluff  
→ Just what accelerates your growth

This means less time stuck.  
More time building, doing, applying.
