# **Failure States**

- Bad mixtures generate **byproducts** like:
    
    - _Cursed Smoke_ – briefly obscures vision or room layout.
        
    - _Slag Puddle_ – blocks a table until cleaned with quicklime.
        
    - _Shadow Ooze_ – harmless but eerie; may hint at wrong [[Combos]].
