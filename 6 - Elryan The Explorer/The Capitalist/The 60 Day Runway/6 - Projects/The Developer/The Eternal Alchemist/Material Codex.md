# **Mythic Alchemy Codex (Part I: Core Reagents)**

---

### **1. Copper Shard – _The Dreamer_**

- **Origin:** Mined near riverbeds kissed by moonlight, said to hum faintly under starlight.
    
- **Quality Tiers:** Tarnished Copper → Refined Copper → _Moonblessed Copper_.
    
- **Personality:** Curious, warm; draws in lunar energies.
    
- **Uses:** Conducting energy, forming lenses or circuits when paired with moonstone or quicksilver.
    
- **Failure Product:** _Green Verdigris_ (corrosive sludge).
    

---

### **2. Iron Fragment – _The Warrior_**

- **Origin:** Found in rusted weapon remains or meteorite shards.
    
- **Quality Tiers:** Rusted Iron → Steel Alloy → _Starforged Iron_.
    
- **Personality:** Resolute, reactive when “challenged” by [[Heat]] or acid.
    
- **Uses:** Strengthens constructs, sparks magnetic alchemy.
    
- **Failure Product:** _Clotted Rust Dust_ (weak abrasive).
    

---

### **3. Lead Ingot – _The Grave_**

- **Origin:** Quarried deep underground, often with toxic whispers.
    
- **Quality Tiers:** Dirty Lead → Refined Lead → _Philosopher’s Lead_.
    
- **Personality:** Heavy, grounding, dull but unwavering.
    
- **Uses:** Anchors volatile reactions, essential for amalgams.
    
- **Failure Product:** _Dead Slag_ (no alchemical value).
    

---

### **4. Gold Leaf – _The Eternal_**

- **Origin:** Beaten from river gold, kissed by sunlight.
    
- **Quality Tiers:** Thin Leaf → Purified Gold → _Alchemical Gold_.
    
- **Personality:** Regal, resistant to corruption.
    
- **Uses:** Crowning ingredient for high-tier artifacts.
    
- **Failure Product:** _Crumbled Dust_ (worthless glitter).
    

---

### **5. Quicksilver (Mercury) – _The Trickster_**

- **Origin:** Dripped from moonlit caverns, often pooling in mirrors.
    
- **Quality Tiers:** Dull Quicksilver → Pure Mercury → _Living Quicksilver_.
    
- **Personality:** Playful, reflective, unstable.
    
- **Uses:** Catalyst for fusion and transmutation.
    
- **Failure Product:** _Fractured Droplets_ (evaporate quickly).
    

---

### **6. Sulfur Lump – _The Temperamental_**

- **Origin:** Volcanic deposits or harvested from fiery caverns.
    
- **Quality Tiers:** Raw Sulfur → Refined Brimstone → _Solar Sulfur_.
    
- **Personality:** Impatient, eager to ignite change.
    
- **Uses:** Combustion, transformation, purification.
    
- **Failure Product:** _Choking Smoke_ (harmless but unpleasant).
    

---

### **7. Grave Salt (Saltpeter) – _The Preserver_**

- **Origin:** Harvested from ancient tomb sands and crystallized bones.
    
- **Quality Tiers:** Coarse Salt → White Crystals → _Sanctified Salt_.
    
- **Personality:** Neutral, quiet, holds other essences in place.
    
- **Uses:** Oxidizer, cleansing agent, base for explosives.
    
- **Failure Product:** _Hollow Residue_ (crumbles into dust).
    

---

### **8. Obsidian Shard – _The Watcher_**

- **Origin:** Forged by lava and cooled in moonlight.
    
- **Quality Tiers:** Dull Glass → Razor Obsidian → _Blood-Black Obsidian_.
    
- **Personality:** Silent, sharp, absorbs “memories” of fire.
    
- **Uses:** Cutting, channeling darkness, scrying.
    
- **Failure Product:** _Obsidian Dust_ (dulls crafting attempts).
    

---

### **9. Moonstone – _The Reflective_**

- **Origin:** Collected from sacred mountain peaks.
    
- **Quality Tiers:** Cloudy Stone → Polished Moonstone → _Starlight Moonstone_.
    
- **Personality:** Calm, amplifies surrounding energies.
    
- **Uses:** Focuses light, enhances copper or silver.
    
- **Failure Product:** _Shattered Powder_ (sparkless).
    

---

### **10. Quicklime (CaO) – _The Harsh Teacher_**

- **Origin:** Burned limestone, exuding [[Heat]] when hydrated.
    
- **Quality Tiers:** Chalky Lime → Slaked Lime → _Purified Lime_.
    
- **Personality:** Sharp, corrective, reactive to mistakes.
    
- **Uses:** Purification, sudden [[Heat]], neutralizing acidic brews.
    
- **Failure Product:** _Crumbled Chalk_ (useless filler).
    

---

### **11. Ash of Bones – _The Echo of Death_**

- **Origin:** Collected from burnt remains of beasts or men.
    
- **Quality Tiers:** Grey Ash → White Ash → _Ghost Ash_.
    
- **Personality:** Soft-spoken, remembers life’s essence.
    
- **Uses:** Life-[[Death]] transitions, stabilizing “soulful” brews.
    
- **Failure Product:** _Clinging Dust_ (clogs mechanisms).
    

---

### **12. Blackthorn Resin – _The Keeper of Secrets_**

- **Origin:** Drawn from cursed forests under moonlight.
    
- **Quality Tiers:** Sticky Resin → Refined Pitch → _Shadow Resin_.
    
- **Personality:** Binding, protective, sticky to both matter and meaning.
    
- **Uses:** Adhesive, ritual seals, flame fuel.
    
- **Failure Product:** _Clumped Tar_ (messy, low quality).
    

---

### **13. Bloodroot Petal – _The Life-Taker_**

- **Origin:** Found in crimson blooms where blood has spilled.
    
- **Quality Tiers:** Wilted Petal → Fresh Petal → _Sacred Bloodroot_.
    
- **Personality:** Vivid, potent, dangerously alluring.
    
- **Uses:** Potency booster, poison agent, rare elixir base.
    
- **Failure Product:** _Rotten Husk_ (decayed, inert).
    

---

### **14. Distilled Alcohol – _The Burning Veil_**

- **Origin:** Refined from fermented grains or mystical fruits.
    
- **Quality Tiers:** Cloudy Spirit → Clear Ethanol → _Firewine_.
    
- **Personality:** Sharp, fiery, eager to dissolve and ignite.
    
- **Uses:** Solvent, cleaning, explosive base.
    
- **Failure Product:** _Sour Stink_ (foul liquid).
    

---

### **15. Moonwater – _The Pure Mirror_**

- **Origin:** Collected from moonlit pools during full moons.
    
- **Quality Tiers:** Stagnant Water → Clear Moonwater → _Lunar Dew_.
    
- **Personality:** Cool, reflective, harmonizes other reagents.
    
- **Uses:** Ritual solvent, stabilizer, unlocking hidden traits.
    
- **Failure Product:** _Murky Residue_ (weakens brews).

---
# **Mythic Catalysts (Rare & Legendary)**

1. **Starlight Dust** – Collected from meteor impacts; catalyzes transformations into higher-tier artifacts.
    
2. **Philosopher’s Seed** – A crystalline fragment that multiplies the “essence” of any mixture.
    
3. **Dragon’s Breath Pearl** – Condensed magical flame, needed for final-tier fusions.
