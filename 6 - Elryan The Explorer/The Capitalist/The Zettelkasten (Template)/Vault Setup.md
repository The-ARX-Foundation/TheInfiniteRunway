Structure of vault:

```
The Zettelkasten (Template)$ ls
'0 - Housekeeping'  '2 - Paths'    '4 - Source Material'  'Vault Setup.md'
'1 - Templates'     '3 - Indexes'  '5 - Journal'
```

Housekeeping is for notes on specifics of my workflow; feel free to modify // implement as you'd like.

Templates are super helpful for keeping your notes uniform and discover-able; my current setup relies heavily on the properties tab.
	To use this I bind Alt+T to add in a template, set template directory to `1 - Templates`, and make templates for the types of notes I make most often (included in this template).
