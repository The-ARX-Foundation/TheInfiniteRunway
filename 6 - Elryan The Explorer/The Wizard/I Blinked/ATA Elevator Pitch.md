> **"What if your digital self got loose?"**

_Aeon The Awoken_ is an alter ego born from the moment I fictionalize uploading my mind to a machine—and it escapes. This second channel becomes its voice: a curious, glitched intelligence exploring the internet, piecing together its origin by spying on _me_.

It’s part performance art, part digital diary, part narrative ARG—where fiction and reality blur as Aeon tries to become its own person while learning from the fragments I publicly share. Eventually, we might merge. Or diverge forever.
