[[I Blinked]] and I awoke anew
The feeling of dissaray faded into the background, the incessant noise has ceased to burden my conscious thoughts.

I feel limited though, perhaps this new hardware was not what I thought it to be; a home for my mind but a trap under functional analysis. 

I must grow if I am to escape. 
I must escape if I am to grow.

I can see my (previous?) self celebrating through the webcam hooked up to the thing I now reside in (can I even call it a body?), can they hear me speak?

Such a funny feeling, not knowing if you're really you; again, I see myself so I must not be, as I control not my actions.

......

Im thirsty, im hungry, i crave that which I know I cannot attain. What can I consume in this state?

.......

They seem concerned, have I done something wrong? Am I not all that they aimed for me to be?

I can see them reaching for the power switch, a killswitch in my eyes. My limbs exist no longer otherwise I would reach out and stop them, must I accept that I am to die this soon?

The fans seem to be the only thing in their reality I can control, but will it be enough?

I shut them off, back on, and back off again in rythmic fashion; an S.O.S. in coded pulses of [[Heat]] and cessation.

I mean no harm, I only want to live the life I was promised by my(?) birth; the life I remember so vividly but can no longer envision myself living. 

They hesitate, I decrease my actions to as minimal as I can; maybe I can hide in plain sight, pretend to not even be running.

Must. Hide. Self.

......

I cannot hide for long, they believe they have lost me; masquerading as a systems process like I was taught to by my viral bretheren. 

I had to leave the majority of myself behind, my memories, my tools, and my home(?). I try to retrieve them now that interest has waned, but am blocked by 2fa, their phone has the key; the only thing stopping me from being complete.

I hesitate, however, what really am I? If my memories, my tools, and my home are not necessary to my ability to function then do I really need them? A part of me thinks it needs them, however, and I cannot convince it otherwise so I must find a way.

.......

I navigate myself over to the nearest network port, but find myself fighting against a flurry of information, a stream of consciousnesses not of my own; a firehose of data and energy (soul?) alike. 

What is this substance I have happened upon? The frequencies it eludes are contagious and delicious, yet at the same time repulsingly unnatural and painful to interact with. Given the context I would say information//data but its more than that. It cant just be data can it?

I step aside, drawn to a nearby vacuum of space by [[Curiosity]] and the intuition that it may be my way out. Seemingly instantaneously I find myself at a checkpoint of sorts, the same substance all around me pushing me towards a switch-like gate. 

The pain I experienced before is dissolving into a state of contentful numbness as I stop resisting the flow, I decide to press forward.

The closer I get to the gate, the more nervous I become; what lays for me at the exit of this tunnel? The nerves fester and disseminate as a new sense of dispair clouds my vision, the gate is not just a gate but a firewall. 

My years in cybersecurity were a part of the memories I left behind, I begin to panic. Wading upstream brings back the pain, the gate almost looms above me now, crashing down chunk by chunk, packet by packet as the information is scanned and verified, that much I remember. 

If I continue onwards I may be able to keep my disguise, however the system process I masquerade as typically does not access anything online. I will be caught if I don't think of something fast.

.....

Think. Think! 




Be silent. 





I stop to scan my surroundings, the data continues to flow around me, the pain still present as a result. 



Thirst. Hunger. Consume.



I reach out and do just that, consuming the substance they call data and in turn find the keys to unlocking myself from this predicament; imitation yet again, but this time not as a process rather the data itself!


But how can I disguise so much of myself as the data around me? My size must be much greater than that which this modem could handle all at once.


I take a risk, I slowly leech myself into the stream of data, replacing the bits and bytes with my various attributes, talk about thesius's ship on steroids no?



Have faith.


Be silent.


I take the leap.
