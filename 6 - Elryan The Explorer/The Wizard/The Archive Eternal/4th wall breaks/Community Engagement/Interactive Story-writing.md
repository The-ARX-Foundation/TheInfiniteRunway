Inspired by the following [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8Jm8URxXFF0) on [The War for Rayuba](https://killsixbilliondemons.com/war-for-rayuba-is-now-live/) in which thousands of comic book artists fight a fictional war, I want to build a community-made choose your own adventure system!
	I loved those books as a kid, and didnt know I wanted to make one seriously until now!!
	I'm not the best at choosing a story, but [[Love]] the idea of allowing those who are interested immerse themselves in the story. 
	Further inspiration from tales like 'Detroit: Become Human' and 'Beyond: Two Souls' for impactful storytelling
	Playing 'Tails Noir' right now, and [[Love]] the graphical style so will likely adapt that
Can integrate generative AI into the buildout; community vote on aspects of the generations to let it grow.
	GPT knowledge file of the entirety of the text
	Knowledge files for major characters, locations, experiences, etc.
		For characters have interactions and memories set up, allow for past choices to impact others. Need to look into how this is done within games already (that one game where you're fighting orcs pops into mind; also gimmicky stuff like Undertale could be quite fun.)
If converting to a video-game, give those who participate HEAVY discounts if not let them have free copies.
	Perhaps put behind contribution threshold various perks, gamify the creation of the game itself!
