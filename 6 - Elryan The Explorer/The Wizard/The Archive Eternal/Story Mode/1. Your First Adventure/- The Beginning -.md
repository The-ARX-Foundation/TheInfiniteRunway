## Storyboard
The game starts with you waking up at some desk job, cubicle, startled by a notification on your desktop; a work order to check out some malfunctioning drive at the data facility
You lock your computer and pack up, ready to complete this task on an otherwise uneventful day.

Introduce some side characters in 'reality' (to be denoted as IRL from now on) such that they are familiar when conversating later
	boss, colleague, etc.

Player navigates to the data center, a vast and liminal space, and finds the drive in question.
	The SATA port looks fried, along with the rest of the drive. Unknown markings are present on the interior of the drive case after prying it open. The disk, however looks fine.

Through some interaction (look into data recovery, likely moving the disk to a new enclosure; mini game and intro to puzzle interactions) finally access the drive, and load into it to ensure it is in proper working order.

Surprising you, you experience arising from the pensieve for the first time as Balthazar, and peer into your [[Reflection]] beneath the ripples of the smokey liquid contained within it.	

A voice sounds within your head, "Testing? Testing?", timid and annoyed as if many attempts have been made to do whatever it is they have been trying to do. 'This is Balthazar -last name- on -date-, attempt number -number- at using this forsaken pensieve.' 

You look down at your hands, your avatar and the body you currently inhabit seem to move out of sync/phase, but the body mimics your movements; a phenomena you have never experienced before now. 

"[[Temporal Sync|Temporal Sync]] seems to be in order, if I will myself so my movements are not my own. My thoughts, however, are still within my volition."

You think about attempting communication, but find yourself unable to speak; for that matter you find yourself unable to feel anything outside of what you are experiencing. A shudder runs down your spine, you see Balthazar walking forwards while your avatar remains still. It seems that the consent of control is a two way street. 

Do you relinquish control?
