# Cell types to know (organized by compartment)

## Ventricular lining (non-plexus)

- **Ependymocytes (multiciliated ependymal cells)**
    
    - Simple columnar/cuboidal, **cilia move CSF**, microvilli for exchange.
        
    - Form **ependyma** (NOT tight junction sealed like CP; more permissive).
        
- **Tanycytes** (specialized ependymal cells; mainly 3rd ventricle floor/hypothalamus)
    
    - Long basal processes into hypothalamus; **transport/signaling** roles (metabolic, neuroendocrine).
        
- **Subventricular zone (SVZ) players (adjacent, not lining):**
    
    - **B1 astrocyte-like stem cells**, **transit-amplifying progenitors**, **neuroblasts** (context for regeneration papers).
        

## Choroid plexus epithelium (the barrier + pump)

- **Choroid plexus epithelial cells** (aka modified ependymal cells)
    
    - **Tight junctions apically** → **BCSFB**.
        
    - Transporters/enzymes for CSF secretion: **Na⁺/K⁺-ATPase, NKCC, carbonic anhydrase, aquaporins (AQP1)** (know the idea, not the alphabet soup).
        
    - **Microvilli** increase surface; some cilia present.
        

## Choroid plexus stroma & vasculature

- **Fenestrated capillary endothelial cells**
    
    - Leaky endothelium; barrier function is **epithelial**, not endothelial here (contrast with BBB).
        
- **Pericytes**
    
    - Vascular stability/regulation.
        
- **Fibroblasts / stromal cells**
    
    - Loose connective tissue core support.
        
- **Resident immune cells**
    
    - **Epiplexus (Kolmer) macrophages** on ventricular side; **CP macrophages** and other leukocytes can traffic via CP; CP secretes **cytokines/chemokines** (neuroinflammation gatekeeper).
        

# Functions you should have in your head

- **CSF production & composition control** (ions, osmotic gradients → water follows).
    
- **Blood–CSF barrier** (tight junction **epithelium** at CP; selective entry/clearance of solutes, xenobiotics, wastes; complements BBB).
    
- **CSF flow support** (ependyma cilia beat → circulation; ties to glymphatic clearance concept).
    
- **Signaling & immune surveillance** (CP secretome: transferrin for iron handling, cytokines that regulate leukocyte entry).
    

# Where things are (testable landmarks)

- **CP locations:**
    
    - **Lateral ventricle** body & atrium (not in anterior horn).
        
    - **Roof of 3rd** (continuous via foramina of Monro).
        
    - **Part of roof of 4th** (with median/lateral apertures nearby).
        
- **Tela choroidea:** pia-ependymal fold forming ventricle roofs (3rd & 4th) and **choroidal fissure** track in lateral ventricle (between fornix & thalamus → surgical corridor).
    
- **Blood supply classics:** **Anterior choroidal a.** (ICA), **posterior choroidal aa.** (PCA), **PICA** contributions in 4th; venous drainage to **internal cerebral veins**.
    

# Clinical hooks (one-liners)

- **Hydrocephalus** (overproduction/flow obstruction/absorption failure; CP papilloma can overproduce).
    
- **Choroid plexus cysts** (fetal US finding; usually benign/resolve).
    
- **CP tumors** (papilloma/carcinoma—peds; CSF overproduction, hydrocephalus).
    
- **Calcified CP** on imaging (often incidental in adults).
    
- **Herniation link** is meninges/ventricle-adjacent (from your Video 4), but CP is key in **CSF dynamics** that influence pressure.
    

# Minimal mental model (say it out loud)

> **Tela choroidea (pia) + ependyma → choroid plexus epithelium (tight junctions) over fenestrated caps → pumps ions/water to make CSF.**  
> **Ependyma elsewhere is ciliated, moves CSF, and is more permeable.**  
> **CP is also an immune & transport gateway distinct from the BBB.**
