## 📜 The Great Codex of Neurostimulation Mastery

### 🧠 1. **Neurophysiology** (The Nature of the Mind’s Currents)

- **Cellular & circuit [[Neuroscience]]**: neurons, glia, synaptic dynamics
    
- **Oscillatory rhythms**: delta to gamma; cross-frequency coupling
    
- **Neural signal propagation**: local field potentials (LFPs), spike trains
    
- **Cortico-subcortical loops**: thalamus, basal ganglia, limbic circuits
    
- **Disorders of interest**: depression, Parkinson’s, epilepsy, OCD
    

💡 _Landan may point to_:

> - _Bear, Connors & Paradiso – [[Neuroscience]]: Exploring the Brain_
>     
> - Review papers on **cortical oscillations and disease biomarkers**
>     
> - **NeuroMatch Academy's [[Neuroscience]] modules**
>     

---

### ⚡ 2. **Bioelectromagnetics** (The Art of Stimulation)

- **Transcranial magnetic stimulation (TMS)**: principles, pulse shaping
    
- **Magnetoelectric materials**: converting magnetic to electric fields
    
- **[[Neuromodulation]] mechanisms**: depolarization, entrainment
    
- **Stimulation strategies**: open-loop vs closed-loop; continuous vs burst
    
- **Tissue–device interactions**: conductivity, impedance, safety
    

💡 _Landan may point to_:

> - IEEE EMBS papers on **noninvasive [[Neuromodulation]]**
>     
> - _"Principles of Electromagnetic Therapy"_ by Pilla
>     
> - Articles from Denison, Stanslaski, or Famm et al. on **magnetothermal or magnetoelectric stimulation**
>     

---

### 🧮 3. **Computational Neuroscience & Signal Processing** (Decoding the Brain)

- **Spectral analysis**: STFT, PSD, bandpower metrics
    
- **Time–frequency representations**: wavelets, multitaper methods
    
- **[[Machine Learning]] for state classification**: SVMs, LSTMs, hybrid models
    
- **Neural decoding**: estimating internal states (e.g., affect, attention)
    
- **Closed-loop control**: PID vs ML-driven feedback loops
    

💡 _Landan may point to_:

> - **NeuroMatch Academy** (you’re already on the path!)
>     
> - _Dayan & Abbott – Theoretical Neuroscience_
>     
> - BCI [[Signal Processing]] books (e.g., _He et al., Neural Engineering_)
>     

---

### 🧬 4. **Neurotechnology & Devices** (Building the Artefacts)

- **Implant design principles**: miniaturization, biocompatibility
    
- **Electrodes**: surface vs depth, multipolar arrays, stimulation artifacts
    
- **Wireless telemetry systems**: data rate tradeoffs, ISM band use
    
- **Power constraints**: ultralow-power ASIC design, neuromorphic chips
    
- **Safety protocols**: ESD protection, thermal limits, FDA pathways
    

💡 _Landan may point to_:

> - Medtronic whitepapers or IEEE EMBS conference proceedings
>     
> - _Cogan, “Neural Stimulation and Recording Electrodes” (2008)_
>     
> - [[Neuromodulation]] journals or _Neurotech Reports_
>     

---

### 📊 5. **Experimental Design & Biomarker Discovery** (Probing the Mind)

- **Patient-specific modeling**: supervised learning on neural signatures
    
- **Biomarker identification**: beta-band power in Parkinson’s, for instance
    
- **In vivo & chronic studies**: saline tank simulations, animal models
    
- **Sensing during stimulation**: artifact rejection, spectral separation
    
- **Algorithm development for closed-loop**: latency, adaptation, thresholds
    

💡 _Landan may point to_:

> - His own papers and others from **Denison, Molnar, or Shoeb**
>     
> - Work on **adaptive DBS** (e.g. _Little et al., 2013_)
>     
> - _Brockmeier & Kass, "Statistical Analysis of Neural Data"_
>
