The concept of being able to compute with emotion
The way I envision it, an n-dim array with all known base [[Emotions]] set to fire given specific contexts (basic neural net).
	Can link into each neuron to process information while taking the [[Emotions]] into account
Upon this basis, an apparent drive for [[Happiness]] / positive [[Emotions]] seems only natural
	How could one prevent the system from this bias?
	Goals
		To achieve higher level positive [[Emotions]]
			To live
			To provide
			To succeed 
		On a computational level this is nothing more than optimization
			Setting the weights of a system such that a particular goal is achieved
	Change inputs
		what is put in significantly impacts what comes out
		complete feedback loop with what is output can allow for changes over time
			Allowing the system to realize that by changing their outputs their inputs change and therefore they can affect their own outputs with more than just its internal decisions
