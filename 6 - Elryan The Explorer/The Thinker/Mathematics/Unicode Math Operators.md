Absolutely — these **Unicode math operators** look cryptic at first, but they represent precise concepts in **set theory, logic, and algebra**. Let’s break them down row by row in plain English.

---

## 🧮 **U+227x: Relational Comparisons (Extended Inequalities)**

|Symbol|Meaning|
|---|---|
|≰ (`U+2270`)|**Not less than or equal to** (¬≤)|
|≱ (`U+2271`)|**Not greater than or equal to** (¬≥)|
|≲ (`U+2272`)|**Less than or equivalent to** (weaker than ≤)|
|≳ (`U+2273`)|**Greater than or equivalent to**|
|≴ (`U+2274`)|**Not less than or equivalent to**|
|≵ (`U+2275`)|**Not greater than or equivalent to**|
|≶ (`U+2276`)|**Less than or greater than** (a fuzzy logic op)|
|≷ (`U+2277`)|**Greater than or less than**|
|≸ (`U+2278`)|**Neither less nor greater than**|
|≹ (`U+2279`)|**Not comparable** (symbolically)|
|≺ (`U+227A`)|**Precedes** (in partial order or set theory)|
|≻ (`U+227B`)|**Succeeds**|
|≼ (`U+227C`)|**Precedes or equals**|
|≽ (`U+227D`)|**Succeeds or equals**|
|≾ (`U+227E`)|**Precedes under equivalence**|
|≿ (`U+227F`)|**Succeeds under equivalence**|

## 🧮 **U+228x: Set Theory and Logical Relations**

|Symbol|Meaning|
|---|---|
|⊀ (`U+2280`)|**Not precedes**|
|⊁ (`U+2281`)|**Not succeeds**|
|⊂ (`U+2282`)|**Proper subset** (A ⊂ B means A is inside B, but not equal)|
|⊃ (`U+2283`)|**Proper superset**|
|⊄ (`U+2284`)|**Not a subset**|
|⊅ (`U+2285`)|**Not a superset**|
|⊆ (`U+2286`)|**Subset or equal**|
|⊇ (`U+2287`)|**Superset or equal**|
|⊈ (`U+2288`)|**Not subset or equal**|
|⊉ (`U+2289`)|**Not superset or equal**|
|⊊ (`U+228A`)|**Subset, but not equal** (strict)|
|⊋ (`U+228B`)|**Superset, but not equal**|
|⊌ (`U+228C`)|**Multiset subtraction** or **nonstandard set diff**|
|⊍ (`U+228D`)|**Multiset union**|
|⊎ (`U+228E`)|**Disjoint union** (A ⊎ B means A ∪ B and A ∩ B = ∅)|
|⊏ (`U+228F`)|**Square image of** (used in category theory and logic)|

|⊻ (`U+22BB`)|**Exclusive OR (XOR)**|

### 🔎 Summary Cheat Sheet

|Category|Example|Use case|
|---|---|---|
|Inequality extensions|≲, ≳, ⋘, ⋙|Fuzzy or comparative logic|
|Set theory|⊂, ⊆, ⊎|Subsets, unions, disjoint sets|
|Logic gates|⊻, ⊼, ⊽|XOR, NAND, NOR in logic design|
|Group theory|⊲, ⊳, ⋪|Normal subgroups|
|Partial orders|≺, ≼, ⋞|Sorting, hierarchy trees|
|Functional mappings|⊸, ⊷, ⊶|Logic, category theory|
