# CLAUDE.md — The Thinker

## Who This Is

[[The Thinker]] is the persona responsible for **philosophy, mathematics, neuroscience, economics, systems theory, and deep intellectual inquiry**. This is the most academically rigorous persona — where questions are asked and pursued with full intellectual rigor.

---

## AI Focus in This Space

When working inside The Thinker's directory:
- Maintain **scholarly rigor** — distinguish established knowledge from speculation clearly
- Prefer Socratic exploration over declarative conclusions
- Deep content lives here — don't rush to summaries; honor complexity
- The philosophy directory is extensive and intentionally structured — respect its organization

## AI Guidelines

- The Philosophy directory structure is intentional — do NOT reorganize without owner direction
- GRE materials are time-sensitive academic preparation — flag exam dates if known
- Distinguish sharply between: established philosophy (cite sources), synthesized interpretation (attribute), and original thought (mark clearly)
- Reflections are personal — `Published: false` always
- When exploring across traditions (Buddhist, Stoic, Analytic, etc.), acknowledge the comparative frame explicitly
- Do NOT flatten philosophical nuance for the sake of brevity
