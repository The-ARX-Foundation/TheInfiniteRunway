Title inspiration: in startup culture only 10% of startups make it off the ground due to a phenomenon called "the valley of [[Death]]"; this results from the startup taking on too much debt to stay afloat without taking drastic measures (diluting funding to even bankruptcy). It may not seem like much to someone in the corporate world; but for someone wanting to start a business this is not an insignificant hurdle. Was the education worth the time and effort? I would like to think it was worth the effort put in whilst at university (SIGNIFICANT EFFORT; graduating with honors and did it all with undiagnosed ADHD and who tf knows what else) though I will die on the hill that it is not worth working the next 10 years of my life at some dead end job. Taking things one step at a time, but wish there was something I could do other than sell my soul or part with my creature comforts.
