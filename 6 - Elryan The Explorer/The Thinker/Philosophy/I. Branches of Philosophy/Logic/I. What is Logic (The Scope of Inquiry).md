At its core, **logic** is the study of the principles and criteria of valid reasoning and argument. It examines how we determine the truth or falsity of statements, how we infer conclusions from premises, and how we avoid [[Contradictions]]. Logic concerns itself with **form** over **content**; its primary concern is whether the reasoning process itself is valid, regardless of the truth of individual premises.

Logic can be divided into two primary modes of reasoning:

- **Deductive reasoning**: Where the conclusion necessarily follows from the premises.
- **Inductive reasoning**: Where the conclusion is likely but not guaranteed based on the premises.

By mastering these, we refine our ability to analyze, construct, and deconstruct arguments.
