**Ethics** is the philosophical study of morality, concerning itself with questions about what is right, what is wrong, and how we ought to act. It seeks to determine the principles and values that should guide human behavior, examining both the actions we take and the character we cultivate. Ethics addresses not only individual conduct but also broader societal norms, justice, and the common good.

At its core, ethics asks:

- What does it mean to live a good life?
- What are our duties to others, to ourselves, and to society?
- How can we resolve moral conflicts in a fair and consistent way?

Ethics also bridges theory and practice, offering not only abstract principles but also guidelines for real-world dilemmas.
