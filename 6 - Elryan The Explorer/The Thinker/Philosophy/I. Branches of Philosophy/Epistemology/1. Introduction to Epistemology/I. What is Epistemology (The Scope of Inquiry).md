Themes: [[Philosophy]]

**Epistemology** is the branch of [[Philosophy]] concerned with the nature, scope, and limits of knowledge. It seeks to answer fundamental questions such as:

- What is knowledge?
- [[How do we acquire knowledge]]?
- What distinguishes knowledge from [[Belief]], opinion, or falsehood?
- Can we ever be certain about anything?

The inquiry begins by differentiating between **justified [[Belief]]** and **mere opinion**. Epistemologists explore what it means to claim that something is true, examining the criteria for knowledge and the processes by which we justify our beliefs. Whether through perception, reasoning, testimony, or [[Introspection]], epistemology interrogates the ways in which humans come to know the world.
