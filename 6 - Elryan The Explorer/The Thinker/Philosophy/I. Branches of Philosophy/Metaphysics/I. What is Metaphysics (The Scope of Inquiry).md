Metaphysics deals with **fundamental questions** about the nature of the universe and existence. It encompasses topics that go beyond the physical, such as:

**Being and Existence**  
    [[What does it mean for something to exist]]? [[Is existence a property]]? [[How can we distinguish between what is and what seems to be]]?
**The Nature of Reality**  
    [[What is the ultimate nature of reality]]? [[Is the world we perceive the true reality, or is there a deeper, hidden reality beneath the surface]]?
**The Problem of Universals**  
    [[Do abstract concepts like goodness, redness, or beauty have an existence independent of the physical world Or are they mere constructs of the mind|Do abstract concepts like "goodness," "redness," or "beauty" have an existence independent of the physical world? Or are they mere constructs of the mind?]]
**Cause and Effect**  
    [[What is causality]]? [[Can one thing truly cause another, or is causality a mental illusion]]? [[How do we account for change in the world]]?
**Time and Space**  
    [[Is time real or an illusion]]? [[Does space exist as a container for objects, or is it a relationship between objects]]? [[Is the future predetermined, or is it open]]?
