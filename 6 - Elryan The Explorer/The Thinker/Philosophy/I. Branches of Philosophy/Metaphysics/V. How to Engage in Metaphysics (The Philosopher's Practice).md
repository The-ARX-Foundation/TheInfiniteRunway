Themes: [[Philosophy]]

- **Contemplate Existence**  
    Engage deeply with your own experience of existence. Reflect on what it means for _you_ to exist. Are you merely a physical entity, or is there more to your being?
- **Analyze the World**  
    Begin questioning the very structure of the world around you. For instance, is time passing, or is it static? Is reality as you perceive it?
- **Study the Masters**  
    Explore the writings of metaphysicians like Plato, Aristotle, Kant, and Leibniz. Their works are like ancient scrolls, filled with insights into the deepest workings of the cosmos.

### Deep Questions (Riddles of Existence)

Q1. Is there a fundamental difference between the mind and the body, or are they two aspects of the same reality?  
Q2. If time is an illusion, how can we explain the apparent progression of events?  
Q3. Does the universe have an ultimate purpose, or is it indifferent to the existence of consciousness?
