## 🌐 The 16 Vocabulary Clusters

### 1. **Approval vs Disapproval**

Words about **praise, [[Blame]], endorsement, or rejection**.

- Everyday examples: _applaud, criticize, [[Shame]], endorse, reject, honor_.
    
- Memory frame: “How do societies lift people up or put them down?”
    

---

### 2. **Confidence & Character Spectrum**

Words describing **courage, [[Hesitation]], prudence, or recklessness**.

- Examples: _bold, shy, cautious, reckless, decisive, hesitant_.
    
- Memory frame: “How people step into (or retreat from) challenges.”
    

---

### 3. **Reasoning & Clarity**

Words tied to **logic, argument strength, clarity, or [[Confusion]]**.

- Examples: _logical, persuasive, murky, clear, rational, misleading_.
    
- Memory frame: “Does this argument cut like glass or fog like smoke?”
    

---

### 4. **Truth & Deception**

Words about **honesty, lying, disguise, or hiddenness**.

- Examples: _lie, disguise, trick, reveal, conceal, fake_.
    
- Memory frame: “Masks vs mirrors.”
    

---

### 5. **Emotion & Attitude**

Words describing **emotional intensity or general mood/outlook**.

- Examples: _calm, fiery, hopeful, cynical, loving, hateful_.
    
- Memory frame: “How a heart beats in the world.”
    

---

### 6. **Conflict & Aggression**

Words about **fighting, [[Hostility]], stubbornness, or argument**.

- Examples: _fight, argue, combative, stubborn, resist, confront_.
    
- Memory frame: “Do people clash or cooperate?”
    

---

### 7. **Power & Authority**

Words about **control, dominance, obedience, or submission**.

- Examples: _rule, obey, dethrone, command, submit, dominate_.
    
- Memory frame: “Who holds the reins?”
    

---

### 8. **Quantity & Quality**

Words describing **amount, intensity, scarcity, or abundance**.

- Examples: _many, few, overflowing, bare, rich, weak_.
    
- Memory frame: “Is there plenty or too little?”
    

---

### 9. **Time & Change**

Words tied to **duration, shifts, permanence, or transience**.

- Examples: _short-lived, lasting, fleeting, eternal, sudden, temporary_.
    
- Memory frame: “Does it last or vanish?”
    

---

### 10. **Society & Culture**

Words about **manners, civility, traditions, and worldliness**.

- Examples: _polite, rude, provincial, global, cultured, ignorant_.
    
- Memory frame: “How does a community behave?”
    

---

### 11. **Practicality & Usefulness**

Words describing **effort, diligence, utility, or laziness**.

- Examples: _useful, pointless, hard-working, lazy, efficient, careless_.
    
- Memory frame: “Is it useful, and how much sweat does it take?”
    

---

### 12. **Health & Strength**

Words about **physical or metaphorical vigor, weakness, or wellness**.

- Examples: _healthy, sick, robust, frail, harmful, nourishing_.
    
- Memory frame: “Does it give life or take it?”
    

---

### 13. **Form & Appearance**

Words describing **style, showiness, plainness, or aesthetic choices**.

- Examples: _flashy, plain, stylish, gaudy, elegant, decorative_.
    
- Memory frame: “How does it look on the surface?”
    

---

### 14. **Speech & Expression**

Words tied to **how people speak, write, or express themselves**.

- Examples: _wordy, brief, eloquent, blunt, noisy, silent_.
    
- Memory frame: “How does the voice come across?”
    

---

### 15. **Morality & Virtue**

Words about **integrity, corruption, ethics, or moral standing**.

- Examples: _honest, corrupt, virtuous, sinful, righteous, deceitful_.
    
- Memory frame: “Does the soul stand tall or bent?”
    

---

### 16. **Movement & Change-of-State**

Words about **shifts, reversals, cycles, or processes**.

- Examples: _grow, fade, waver, collapse, replace, evolve_.
    
- Memory frame: “What is in motion, what is in flux?”
