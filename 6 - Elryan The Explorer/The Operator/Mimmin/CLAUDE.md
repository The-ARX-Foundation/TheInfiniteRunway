# CLAUDE.md — Mimmin

## What This Is

**[[Mimmin]]** (play on "mimic" — it cuts your mimic time short) is a self-hosted, always-watching workstation intelligence product. A second brain that auto-refactors your thoughts into categorized filing. Zettelkasten that builds itself.

## Philosophy

- Sovereignty over your own cognition layer — your data, your hardware, no kill switch
- Local-first, privacy-respecting, open-source foundations
- The system handles its own continuity — once running, no more manual transfer docs

## Project Status

**Phase:** Early concept / transfer documentation. The transfer doc captures the full product vision for context handoff.

## Key Concepts

- **Always-watching:** Observes workstation activity and auto-categorizes
- **Self-hosted:** Runs on your hardware, no cloud dependency
- **Auto-continuity:** Replaces manual Claude transfer docs with persistent system memory
- **Zettelkasten native:** Outputs structured notes with frontmatter, wikilinks, and tags
