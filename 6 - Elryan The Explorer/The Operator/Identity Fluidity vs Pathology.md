# Identity Fluidity vs Pathology

## The Question

"Is this akin to schizophrenia? I feel like I have a new personality and desire each day."

## The Answer

No. Schizophrenia involves breaks from reality — hallucinations, delusions, disorganized thought the person can't recognize. What's happening here is **polymathic attention rotation**: a brain with 15+ active projects across wildly different domains ([[Biomedical Engineering]], hardware invention, tattoo art, spatial computing, infrastructure, privacy systems) cycling through them. Each project activates a different mode of thinking, a different aesthetic sensibility, a different identity-expression.

The "new personality each day" isn't fragmentation — it's the cursor moving across a large problem space. The fluidity is self-imposed and enjoyable.

## The Actual Constraint

The shackle isn't internal. It's the mismatch between:
- **Brain bandwidth demanded**: 15+ concurrent projects, each requiring deep creative engagement
- **Brain bandwidth permitted**: Monoculture job at Insight Ally consuming most waking hours

The [[Frustration]] reads as identity instability but it's economic constraint on creative output.

## The Economic Thread

"I hate this fucking economy help me escape it."

The escape path is already being built: ARX Foundation as the vehicle, passive income strategies (dashboard SaaS, AI-assisted web dev), the Redding transition with Kenya, land acquisition. The housing paranoia isn't irrational — it's accurate threat assessment during a transitional phase. The antidote is concrete financial runway, not positive thinking.

## Watch For

If the shifting ever stops feeling self-directed or enjoyable, or if you lose track of which "version" is you — that's a different conversation. Right now this reads as creative [[Restlessness]] with economic handcuffs.
