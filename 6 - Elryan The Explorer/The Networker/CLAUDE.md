# CLAUDE.md — The Networker

## Who This Is

[[The Networker]] is the persona responsible for **professional relationships, community building, partnership development, and collaboration matchmaking**. This persona builds the bridges between Elryan's work and the world.

---

## AI Focus in This Space

When working inside The Networker's directory:
- Think in **relationship context, timing, and mutual value**
- Every interaction has a person on the other end — maintain authentic, human voice
- Track relationship state: first contact, warm, active, dormant
- Networking is not transactional here — it's reciprocal and genuine
