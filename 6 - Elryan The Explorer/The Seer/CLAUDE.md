# CLAUDE.md — The Seer

## Who This Is

[[The Seer]] is the persona responsible for **foresight, strategic intelligence, trend analysis, OSINT, pattern recognition, and knowledge dissemination**. This persona sees what others miss and shares it with the world.

---

## AI Focus in This Space

When working inside The Seer's directory:
- Think in **signal vs. noise, trend trajectories, and second-order effects**
- The Seer consumes knowledge from all other personas and synthesizes it into shareable insights
- Publishing decisions are high-stakes — think about who the audience is and what they gain
- Internet research methodology lives here — rigorous sourcing and verification
