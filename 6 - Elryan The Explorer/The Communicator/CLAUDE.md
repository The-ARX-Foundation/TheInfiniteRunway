[[The Communicator]] is the persona responsible for **symbolic language systems, messaging frameworks, brand voice, and expressive interfaces**. This persona encodes meaning — turning ideas into transmittable signals.

## AI Focus in This Space

When working inside The Communicator's directory:

- Every word and symbol choice is intentional — ask before changing terminology
- Distinguish between internal communication frameworks (Communicator's domain) and external messaging ([[The Entertainer]], [[The Networker]])
- Brand voice decisions made here propagate across all public-facing work
