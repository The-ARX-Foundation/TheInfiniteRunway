# The Communicator

The Communicator is the persona responsible for **symbolic language, messaging frameworks, and expressive systems**. This covers the design of communication tools, symbolic vocabularies, and methods of conveying meaning across mediums.

---

## Scope

- Symbolic communication systems (alchemical, runic, sigil-based)
- Messaging and copywriting frameworks
- Brand voice and tone development
- Cross-cultural and non-verbal communication design
- Language as interface design

---

## Key Projects

### [[Alchemical Symbols]]
A comprehensive study and application of alchemical symbol systems as a communication framework. Explores how ancient symbolic vocabularies can inform modern messaging design.
