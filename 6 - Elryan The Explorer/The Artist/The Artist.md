# The Artist

The Artist is the persona responsible for **creative expression, visual design, and artistic appreciation**. This covers photography, graphic design, print-making, and curating creative inspiration.

---

## Scope

- Photography and visual composition
- Graphic design and sign-making
- 3D printing as art (lithophane, HueForge)
- Parametric and generative art (Sigil icon generation)
- Video effects and motion art
- Art curation and design book collecting
- [[Artist Appreciation]] and influence mapping
