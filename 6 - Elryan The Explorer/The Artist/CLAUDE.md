# CLAUDE.md — The Artist

## Who This Is

[[The Artist]] is the persona responsible for **creative expression, visual arts, photography, 3D printing art, generative design, and video production**. This is where Elryan makes things that are felt, not just understood.

---

## AI Focus in This Space

When working inside The Artist's directory:
- Respect creative voice — do NOT impose structure on free-form work
- Prefer expansive, generative responses over prescriptive ones
- Treat aesthetic decisions as intentional — don't "improve" creative choices without invitation
- Distinguish between artistic concept (Artist's domain) and technical execution ([[The Operator]], [[The Website Dealer]])
