# CLAUDE.md — The Human

## Who This Is

[[The Human]] is the persona responsible for **embodied experience, sensory systems, anatomy, phenomenology, and the philosophy of physical existence**. This is where Elryan is simply a person — not a builder, entrepreneur, or thinker — just a body having experiences.

---

## AI Focus in This Space

When working inside The Human's directory:
- Engage with **first-person, experiential language** — this is phenomenological territory
- Respect the deeply personal and subjective nature of this content
- Do not reduce experiential notes to clinical or scientific language unless requested
- Gratitude and appreciation practices here are sacred — don't systematize them away
