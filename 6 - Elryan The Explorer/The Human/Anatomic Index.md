## 🧠 **1. [[Nervous System]]**

**Function:** [[Communication]], [[sensation]], [[coordination]], [[consciousness]], [[perception]], [[reflexes]], and [[integration of bodily systems]].

---

### 🧩 **Subdivisions of the Nervous System**

#### **[[Central Nervous System (CNS)]]**

- Processes, interprets, and stores information; issues instructions.
    

**Components:**

- **[[Brain]]**
    
    - [[Cerebrum]] — higher functions: [[thought]], [[emotion]], [[language]], [[voluntary movement]]
        
    - [[Cerebral cortex]] — the outer layer of grey matter; site of [[conscious awareness]]
        
    - [[Cerebellum]] — [[balance]], [[coordination]], [[motor learning]]
        
    - [[Brainstem]] — connects brain to spinal cord; controls [[breathing]], [[heart rate]], [[consciousness]]
        
        - Includes [[midbrain]], [[pons]], and [[medulla oblongata]]
            
    - **Specialized Structures:**
        
        - [[Thalamus]] — sensory relay station
            
        - [[Hypothalamus]] — homeostasis, emotion, hormone signaling
            
        - [[Pituitary gland]] — hormonal control center
            
- **[[Spinal cord]]**
    
    - Transmits signals to/from brain
        
    - Controls simple reflexes via [[spinal reflex arcs]]
        

---

#### **[[Peripheral Nervous System (PNS)]]**

- Transmits signals between the CNS and the body.
    

**Divided into:**

- **[[Sensory (afferent) division]]** — detects stimuli from body/environment
    
- **[[Motor (efferent) division]]** — sends commands to [[muscles]] and [[glands]]
    

**Components:**

- **[[Cranial nerves]] (I–XII)** — emerge from brain, serve head/neck (and some internal organs)
    
    - e.g., [[Optic nerve (II)]] for [[vision]], [[Vagus nerve (X)]] for parasympathetic innervation of organs
        
- **[[Spinal nerves]]** — emerge from spinal cord, serve trunk/limbs
    
- **[[Ganglia]]** — clusters of [[neurons]] outside CNS
    
- **[[Plexuses]]** — networks of nerves (e.g., [[brachial plexus]], [[lumbar plexus]])
    

---

#### **[[Autonomic Nervous System (ANS)]]**

- Controls **involuntary** functions like [[digestion]], [[heartbeat]], [[pupil dilation]]
    

**Subdivided into:**

- **[[Sympathetic nervous system]]** — fight or flight: ↑heart rate, ↑respiration, ↓digestion
    
- **[[Parasympathetic nervous system]]** — rest and digest: ↑digestion, ↓heart rate
    
- **[[Enteric nervous system]]** — localized to GI tract, sometimes called the "second brain"
    

---

### 👁️ **Sensory Systems** _(Interface between body & nervous system)_

#### **[[Somatosensory system]]**

- Detects [[touch]], [[temperature]], [[pain]], [[proprioception]]
    
- Receptors located in [[skin]], [[joints]], and [[muscles]]
    

#### **[[Visual system]]** — 🧿 (you mentioned interest here!)

- **[[Eyes]]**
    
    - [[Cornea]], [[Pupil]], [[Lens]], [[Retina]], [[Iris]]
        
    - [[Retina]] houses [[photoreceptors]]:
        
        - [[Rods]] (low light, grayscale)
            
        - [[Cones]] (color, detail)
            
    - Signals travel via:
        
        - [[Optic nerve (Cranial Nerve II)]] → [[Optic chiasm]] → [[Visual cortex]] (in [[occipital lobe]])
            

#### **[[Auditory system]]**

- **[[Ears]]**
    
    - [[Outer ear]], [[Tympanic membrane]], [[Ossicles]], [[Cochlea]]
        
    - [[Hair cells]] in cochlea transduce sound to nerve signals
        
    - Signals travel via [[Auditory nerve]] to [[Temporal lobe]]
        

#### **[[Vestibular system]]**

- Senses [[balance]], [[spatial orientation]], and [[motion]]
    
- Resides in [[inner ear]] (semicircular canals + otolith organs)
    

#### **[[Olfactory system]]**

- **[[Nasal cavity]]** detects airborne chemicals
    
- [[Olfactory receptors]] → [[Olfactory bulb]] → [[Limbic system]] (emotion, memory)
    

#### **[[Gustatory system]]**

- **[[Tongue]]**, [[taste buds]], [[cranial nerves]] (VII, IX, X)
    
- Transmits [[taste]] to [[gustatory cortex]]
    

---

### 🔄 **Integration & Reflexes**

- **[[Reflex arc]]** — simplest nerve circuit: sensory input → interneuron → motor output (e.g., knee-jerk)
    
- **[[Neuroplasticity]]** — brain's ability to rewire based on experience or damage
    
- **[[Neurotransmitters]]** — chemical messengers (e.g., [[dopamine]], [[serotonin]], [[acetylcholine]])
