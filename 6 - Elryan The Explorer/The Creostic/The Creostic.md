# The Creostic

The Creostic is the persona responsible for **philosophical framework development, spiritual exploration, and [[Belief]] system [[Architecture]]**. This is the home of [[Creosticism]] - a personal philosophical system centered on creation, recursive meaning-making, and the act of forming one's own beliefs.
