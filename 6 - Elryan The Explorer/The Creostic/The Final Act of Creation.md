The act of uncorrupted creation holds the highest value. To do so, create until you cannot any longer, striving for perfection within your own view; then leave it for [[Death]] to unveil. (How else can you prevent worldly desires from interfering than to not relish in the consequences of what you create?)

Until you get to this point, of course release what you create! It helps you achieve that perfection, and allows for the inspiration of others. By releasing what you create, you live on through your impacts.
