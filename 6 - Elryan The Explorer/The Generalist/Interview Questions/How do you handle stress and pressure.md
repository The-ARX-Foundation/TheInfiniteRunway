## 🧘 **Key Points to Include**

> “I handle stress by combining physiological and practical strategies. If I feel pressure rising, I use a simple reset like the physiological sigh to calm down and refocus. I also know when to ask for help — especially in regulated environments where accuracy is critical. This way, I can maintain focus and consistency without letting stress compromise the quality of my work.”
