## 🏆 **Greatest Professional Achievement (STAR Version)**

**Situation:**  
In my undergraduate research, our lab received a novel adhesive material from the materials science department that needed cytotoxicity validation. The challenge was that there was no existing protocol or literature on how to test this specific adhesive type.

**Task:**  
I was responsible for designing a way to evaluate its cytotoxicity in mammalian cells, while ensuring compliance with ISO 10993 biocompatibility standards.

**Action:**  
I drafted and refined a full culturing and cytotoxicity testing protocol, drawing on my coursework but adapting it into a reproducible, lab-ready SOP. To run the assays, I also designed and laser-fabricated a custom bioinert testing rig that allowed both direct and indirect cell exposure assays. I then maintained long-term L929 cell cultures under sterile conditions, performed exposure studies, and ensured replicability by cryopreserving lines.

**Result:**  
The protocol I developed was adopted by the lab and is still used today as the standard practice for adhesive cytotoxicity testing. It not only enabled the evaluation of that novel adhesive but also created a reproducible workflow for future biomaterials research. For me, the lasting impact of leaving behind a protocol that continues to guide the lab is one of my proudest achievements.
