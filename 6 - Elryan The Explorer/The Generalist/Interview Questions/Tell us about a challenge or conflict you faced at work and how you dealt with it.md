## 🔬 **Adhesive Cytotoxicity Study**

- **Challenge:** No prior literature existed on how to test this adhesive for cytotoxicity.
    
- **Your action:** You designed a custom laser-cut, bioinert testing rig and adapted ISO 10993 protocols for novel material testing.
    
- **Result:** Enabled your lab to validate a material that otherwise couldn’t have been tested; your protocol is _still in use_ for culturing best practices.  
    👉 _This demonstrates resourcefulness when facing ambiguity._

## 🩺 **Chorioanchor v2 — Fetoscopic Anchoring Tool**

- **Challenge:** The shape memory polymer concept seemed promising, but your tests revealed it actually weakened anchoring.
    
- **Your action:** You built rigs to test thermal cycling and tissue anchoring, gathered quantitative data, and presented findings that contradicted initial assumptions.
    
- **Result:** Project pivoted away from a non-viable path, saving resources.  
    👉 _This shows integrity in reporting negative results and resilience in course-correcting._

## 🫀 **Atrial Shunt for HLHS Patients**

- **Challenge:** Needed a novel electrosurgical tool that could cut and cauterize tissue inside a delicate fetal heart.
    
- **Your action:** You prototyped a flexible PCB-based device, manually soldered leads, and validated on ex vivo beef heart tissue despite limited resources.
    
- **Result:** Proof-of-concept demonstrated feasibility; handed off to underclassmen with clear protocols.  
    👉 _This shows problem-solving under technical and resource constraints._

## 🧹 **Lab Cleanup & Infrastructure Overhaul**

- **Challenge:** The lab was cluttered and inefficient, limiting experimental throughput.
    
- **Your action:** You reorganized equipment, set up new laser-cutting/microscopy/3D printing stations, and streamlined the workflow.
    
- **Result:** Freed two desks, increased lab capacity, and improved project efficiency.  
    👉 _This shows initiative in improving systems beyond your assigned tasks._

## 📊 **Cardiac Data Science (Ongoing)**

- **Challenge:** Clinical data was fragmented and schema-incompatible.
    
- **Your action:** You shadowed clinicians, mapped workflows, and began designing a schema for compatibility.
    
- **Result:** Early steps toward a system that bridges clinical practice with analytical pipelines.  
    👉 _This shows strategic thinking and systems design ability._
