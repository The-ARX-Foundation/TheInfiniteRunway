Good question — here’s how to handle the **general “Why should we hire you?”** in a way that is confident, specific, and ties directly to what they care about (cGMP compliance, precision, and diagnostics impact).

---

## 🧪 **Core Structure**

1. **Match their needs** → precision, SOP compliance, consistency under repetition.
    
2. **Show your skills** → lab protocols, fabrication, cross-disciplinary work.
    
3. **Tie to impact** → diagnostics = patient outcomes.
    

---

## ⚗️ **Full Answer (bioMérieux-tailored)**

> “You should hire me because I bring both the technical precision and the process discipline this role demands. In my undergraduate research, I developed protocols that are still in use, designed custom lab rigs where none existed, and maintained long-term sterile cultures without contamination — all of which required consistency, attention to detail, and adherence to standards. Beyond the technical side, I understand that in diagnostics every buffer, every reagent, and every record contributes to patient health and safety. I want to apply my skills in an environment where that impact is tangible, and I know I can contribute to the team’s reliability and quality from day one.”

---

## 🧩 **Concise Version (30 seconds)**

> “Because I combine precision in lab work with experience developing reproducible protocols and working under standards. I know how to maintain accuracy over repetitive tasks, and I want to bring that consistency to reagent formulation where it directly impacts patient care.”

---

## 🔬 **Bullet Point Version (Memory Jogger)**

- Experience designing and implementing **protocols that last** (SOP mindset).
    
- Skilled in **precision tasks** → pipetting, sterile culture, fabrication.
    
- Comfortable with **cGMP/regulated environments**.
    
- Proven ability to **spot problems and pivot** (e.g., shape memory anchor project).
    
- Motivated by **impact on patient outcomes** through diagnostics.
