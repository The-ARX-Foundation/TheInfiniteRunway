- **[[Tell us about yourself]].**  
    → Craft an origin story that’s relevant and memorable.
    
- **[[What are your strengths and weaknesses]]?**  
    → Avoid clichés like "I'm a perfectionist." Be real and specific.
    
- **Why do you want to work here?**  
    → Show you understand the company’s mission, culture, and impact.
	- [[Why do you want to work here - bioMérieux]]
    
- **Where do you see yourself in 5 years?**  
    → Align your goals with the role’s growth path.
    - [[Where do you see yourself in 5 years - bioMérieux]]
    
- **Why should we hire you ?**  
    → Highlight your unique mix of skills, experience, and mindset.
    - [[Why should we hire you - bioMerieux]]
    
- **[[Tell us about a challenge or conflict you faced at work and how you dealt with it]].**  
    → Use STAR format: Situation, Task, Action, Result.
    - [[Tell me about a time you handled a mistake]]
    
- **What is your greatest professional achievement?**  
    → Pick something measurable, high-impact, and relevant.
    - [[Greatest professional achievement - bioMerieux]]
    
- **[[Why are you leaving your current job]]?**  
    → Stay positive. Focus on growth, learning, or value alignment.
    
- **[[How do you handle stress and pressure]]?**  
    → Share real tactics or examples—don’t say “I don’t get stressed.”
    - [[How do you handle stress and pressure - bioMerieux]]
    
- **Do you have any questions for us?**  
    → Always say yes. Ask thoughtful, role-specific questions.
