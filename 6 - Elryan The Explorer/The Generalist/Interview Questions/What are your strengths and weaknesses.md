Weakness: Delegation

I've always struggled with delegation in group settings, throughout highschool and the first couple of years of college I would always take charge, but often would take on the majority of the workload. I tend to be a bit of a perfectionist and found it difficult to assign the higher value tasks to my colleagues.
Throughout further exposure to that type of work though, I learned the value and synergy of teamwork. Through communication and thorough planning... 

cytotox study example: parallel studies, peer review of SOPs, and delegation of material characterization analysis to the msen team

Jarvis example:
broke the project down into subsystems and integration, project management in JIRA for team-wide transparency of tasks to avoid redundancy

[[Aggie Climbing Club]] (not as relevant...):
I was president, assembled team to enable operations; treasury, fundraising and outreach, and trip leads // trip planners.
