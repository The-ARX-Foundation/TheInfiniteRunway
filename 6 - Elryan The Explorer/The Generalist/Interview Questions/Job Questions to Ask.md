Ah, a most excellent inquiry, Seeker. Below is a curated list of the **[[Interview Questions]]** presented in the video, alongside the **strategic reasoning** behind each—like tools in a wizard’s kit, each designed for different kinds of insight.

---

## 🧭 Strategy Questions (Opening Moves)

These set the stage and help you understand the role’s context and alignment with your skills.

### 1. **Why is the job open?**

**Purpose**: Reveals strategic context—backfill vs new role; helps tailor your answers to the company’s current needs or challenges.

### 2. **Why is this role important to you?**

**Purpose**: Exposes the interviewer’s priorities and emotional investment in the role, allowing you to mirror their concerns.

### 3. **How does this role contribute to the success of the department?**

**Purpose**: Connects the role to departmental objectives—helps position yourself as a missing puzzle piece.

### 4. **What’s one thing your department does particularly well?**

**Purpose**: Aligns your strengths with theirs; reveals culture or process maturity.

### 5. **What’s one thing the department doesn’t do well?**

**Purpose**: Allows you to position your skills as the solution to a pain point.

---

## 📈 Career Growth Questions (Midgame Insight)

These probe the longevity and advancement potential of the role.

### 6. **What would I need to do in my first year to earn “exceeds expectations”?**

**Purpose**: Clarifies success metrics early; screens for unrealistic or undefined expectations.

### 7. **What made the last person in this role successful (or not)?**

**Purpose**: Gives you a blueprint (or warning) of what works and what doesn’t; reverse-engineers expectations.

### 8. **What competencies or skills are you hoping the next person brings that the last one didn’t?**

**Purpose**: Helps you focus your pitch on gaps they're eager to fill.

### 9. **What are the potential long-term growth opportunities here?**

**Purpose**: Assesses whether the company offers upward mobility or is a career cul-de-sac.

### 10. **Tell me about the last person promoted in this group.**

**Purpose**: Reveals whether upward movement is theoretical or real—beware if they’ve never promoted anyone.

---

## 🏛️ Company Culture Questions (Organizational Weather)

These uncover systemic risk and alignment with your values.

### 11. **What’s an issue or concern that keeps you up at night?**

**Purpose**: Surfaces hidden tensions, resourcing problems, or leadership worries.

### 12. **How is your company responding to your competitor’s latest product?**

**Purpose**: Tests strategic awareness and market reactivity; weak answers may signal stagnation.

### 13. **If you could change one thing about the company, what would it be?**

**Purpose**: Reveals cultural or structural red flags; subtle way to expose dysfunction.

---

## 🧙 Hiring Manager Fit (The Human Element)

These questions determine whether you’ll thrive under their leadership.

### 14. **How long have you worked here? What’s kept you so long?**

**Purpose**: Loyalty indicator; tests whether the company truly retains talent.

### 15. **What got you to sign the dotted line?** (for new managers)

**Purpose**: Fresh insight into recent company strengths that attracted a new leader.

### 16. **If I asked your team how you lead, what would they say?**

**Purpose**: Gauges self-awareness and leadership style; helps determine management alignment.

### 17. **What metrics will I be measured against in this role?**

**Purpose**: Clarifies performance evaluation; sanity-checks whether KPIs are realistic.

---

## ⚠️ If There Were Layoffs (Red Flag Ward)

Use only if your research reveals past instability.

### 18. **Why were people laid off in the past?**

**Purpose**: Understand decision-making rationale and whether the same risks remain.

### 19. **What’s changed in the business since then?**

**Purpose**: Tests whether they’ve truly fixed the underlying problems.

### 20. **What commitments are in place to prevent future layoffs?**

**Purpose**: Screens for stability and leadership accountability.

---

## 🎯 Bonus Personalization Question

A subtle yet powerful query to collect feedback:

### 21. **What about my resume stood out to you?**

**Purpose**: Learn what’s working in your pitch; gives insight into their selection criteria and how to replicate success.
