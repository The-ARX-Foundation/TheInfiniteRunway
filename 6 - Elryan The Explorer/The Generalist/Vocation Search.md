## 🔧 **Suggested Additions to Your Process**

### ✅ **Step 0: Define Constraints & Core Needs**

Before listing skills:

- **Minimum Salary Requirement**
    
- **Work Mode Preference** (remote, hybrid, on-site)
    
- **Geographic Constraints** (visa issues, relocation willingness)
    
- **Time Constraints** (e.g., wife's grad program support timeline)
    
- **Healthcare / Stability needs** (e.g., insurance, predictable hours)
    

_Why_: This anchors your search to non-negotiables. It'll help quickly filter out incompatible roles.

---

### 📄 **Step 1.5: Translate Skills to Outcomes**

After listing all your skills, add a column for:

- **Outcomes you've driven with each skill**  
    (e.g., “Reduced prototyping time by 50%,” “Built AI pipeline to convert notes into tasks,” etc.)
    

_Why_: Outcomes are what employers _actually_ hire for. This will help you write stronger applications and tailor your story.

---

### 🧠 **Step 2.5: Build a Personal Role Thesis**

Create a few "role archetypes" that emerge from your skills and desires:

- e.g., “Innovation Generalist in Healthtech,” “Rapid Prototyping Engineer for AI Tools,” “Tech Ethicist / Design Researcher in EdTech”
    

_Why_: This will clarify your narrative to others and help them think of _adjacent roles_ you might not consider.

---

### 📬 **Step 3.5: Build an Outreach & Referral Map**

For each target company/role:

- Do you know anyone there?
    
- Do you know someone who knows someone there?
    
- Can your references write you a warm intro?
    

_Why_: Warm intros drastically boost your chances. This also makes your GPT idea stronger if it surfaces relevant mutual connections.

---

### 🛠️ **Step 4: Application Infrastructure**

- **Track everything** (Notion, Airtable, Sheet, or Obsidian Vault w/ status columns)
    
- Pre-draft flexible resume + cover letter blocks (plug-and-play)
    
- Optional: Build a **1-pager PDF portfolio** for non-technical folks
    

_Why_: Saves time, reduces friction, helps you batch apply and follow up easily.

---

### 🧪 **Step 5: Run Experiments**

- Send out 5–10 diverse applications (dream job vs. safe job vs. long shot)
    
- Measure: Responses, interviews, feedback
    
- Tweak story, resume, and roles accordingly
    

_Why_: Early feedback helps you recalibrate before fully committing to a single path.
