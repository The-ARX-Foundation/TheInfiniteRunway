# Persona Org Charts — The 20 CEOs of Elryan

Each Elryan persona operates as a CEO with a team of sub-personas (supporting characters). These aren't separate people — they're **functional roles** that activate when the persona-CEO is in the chair. Think of them as hats within hats.

The naming convention follows the vault's mythic tone: titles are archetypal, not corporate. Each sub-persona gets a one-line mandate and a short description of what they actually do.

---

## Cross-Cutting Roles

Some sub-personas serve multiple CEOs. These are **shared services** in the Elryan org:

| Shared Role | Serves | Function |
|---|---|---|
| **The Archivist** | All 20 | Ensures every decision, artifact, and insight is captured in the Zettelkasten |
| **The Entity Router** | Capitalist, Gatekeeper, Operator | Routes work to the correct legal entity (ARX / [[Hopefully Abysmal]] / Personal) |
| **The Voice** | Communicator, Entertainer, Wizard | Maintains consistent narrative voice across creative and public-facing work |
| **The Bridge** | Generalist, Networker, Professor | Facilitates knowledge transfer between personas and external collaborators |
| **The Sovereign** | Operator, Defender, Gatekeeper | The self-hosted infrastructure layer — sovereign.local, the digital fortress |

## How This Maps to Sovereign

In [[Sovereign Setup]], each persona is registered as an org. Sub-personas could become:
- **Agent roles** within each org (sovereign agent framework)
- **Project labels** for task routing
- **Prompt personas** for context-switching within sovereign's AI layer

The sub-personas are the workforce. The CEO-personas are the decision-makers. Sovereign is the office building they all work in.
