# The Generalist

The Generalist is the meta-persona and central hub of the [[Elryan The Explorer]] system. This persona manages **career strategy, cross-domain synthesis, and the progressive overload of knowledge across all other personas**.

---

## Scope

- [[Vocation Search]] and career navigation
- [[Job Applications]] and interview preparation
- Resume development and professional branding
- PhD exploration and academic trajectory planning
- Progressive overload methodology across disciplines
- Cross-persona coordination and synthesis

## Role in the System

The Generalist is where all personas converge. When a project doesn't clearly belong to one persona, it starts here. When career decisions require balancing multiple personas, The Generalist mediates. This is the persona that asks: "What should Elryan be doing next?"
