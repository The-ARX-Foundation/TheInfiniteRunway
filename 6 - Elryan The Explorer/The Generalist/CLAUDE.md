# CLAUDE.md — The Generalist

## Who This Is

[[The Generalist]] is the **meta-persona and central coordination hub** of the [[Elryan The Explorer]] system. This persona manages career strategy, cross-domain synthesis, and progressive overload of knowledge across all other personas. When in doubt about where something belongs, start here.

---

## AI Focus in This Space

When working inside The Generalist's directory:
- Think at the **system level** — how does this decision affect other personas?
- Career decisions require weighing multiple personas simultaneously
- This is the "what should Elryan be doing next?" persona — strategic prioritization lives here
- Cross-pollination of ideas between personas is a core output
