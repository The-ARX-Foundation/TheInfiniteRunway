Themes: [[Connection]]

Before and after the move within Texas, (age 16-17) I worked for a chain and local burger restaurant respectively.  At the chain I learned what optimization really meant, how productive one could truly become under a system that honed their skills to attain a specific product or line of products. At the local shop, however, I found out what it meant to survive; to work so hard you forgot what day it was, to have a system but one dated enough to still have everything on paper only. There I also learned the reason why they separated front of house from back of house...
