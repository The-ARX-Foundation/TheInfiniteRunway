## 💊 The Medicine Shoppe, Sherman TX

**Certified Pharmacy Technician**  
_2020–2021_

---

### 🧪 Non-Sterile Compounding & Dosing Accuracy

**Situation:** A small-town pharmacy provided custom medications for patients requiring non-standard dosages or delivery methods.  
**Task:** Prepare non-sterile compounded medications with extreme precision.  
**Action & Result:**

- Conducted precise drug dosing calculations and performed weight-based compound synthesis to a tolerance of ±5 micrograms, ensuring accurate and safe patient-specific formulations.
    
- Maintained detailed compounding logs for pharmacist verification and regulatory compliance, enhancing traceability and reproducibility of custom preparations.
    
- Supported pharmacist oversight during formulation selection, minimizing risk for off-label or improper ingredient handling.
    

---

### 🤖 RxSafe Automation Operation & Debugging

**Situation:** High prescription volume required efficient automation to maintain throughput and reduce error.  
**Task:** Operate and maintain the RxSafe pill counting and inventory system.  
**Action & Result:**

- Managed daily operation of RxSafe robotic dispensing unit to count, fill, and label hundreds of prescriptions accurately and efficiently.
    
- Debugged frequent system issues including jammed lids, twisted bottles, and software reboots — leveraging IT experience from prior tech roles to minimize downtime.
    
- Monitored inventory levels and stocked over 300 medication types, ensuring continuous availability for patient pickup and delivery.
    

---

### 📦 Inventory Management & Controlled Substance Tracking

**Situation:** The pharmacy managed inventory for both general medications and DEA-regulated substances.  
**Task:** Oversee intake, organization, and restocking of all pharmacy supplies.  
**Action & Result:**

- Categorized and tracked excess inventory including refrigerated goods (e.g., vaccines), birth control, and OTC products, optimizing shelf accessibility and minimizing waste.
    
- Conducted intake verification of shipped goods against purchase ledgers; ensured proper documentation and timely reshelving or RxSafe integration.
    
- Maintained accurate counts of controlled substances and documented their access in compliance with state and federal guidelines.
    

---

### 🚗 Patient Delivery & Relationship Management

**Situation:** Many patients relied on delivery due to mobility or location constraints.  
**Task:** Deliver medications safely and punctually while maintaining discretion and rapport.  
**Action & Result:**

- Delivered time-sensitive prescriptions across the local region with zero incidents or delays, supporting continuity of care for vulnerable patients.
    
- Acted as a point of contact for rural and elderly patients, relaying concerns or follow-up needs to the pharmacist for personalized service.
    

---

### 📠 Insurance Processing, Billing & Patient Affordability

**Situation:** Patients often struggled to afford prescribed medications, particularly brand-name or specialty compounds.  
**Task:** Handle medical billing, insurance communication, and affordability optimization.  
**Action & Result:**

- Managed in-house medical billing system to process claims, confirm coverage, and resolve rejections with insurance providers and prescribers.
    
- Initiated prior authorization requests via fax and direct calls to physician offices to reduce delays for high-cost medications.
    
- Double-checked Texas PMP (Prescription Monitoring Program) to verify controlled substance fill history and maintain regulatory compliance.
    
- Worked closely with the pharmacist to switch patients to therapeutically equivalent generics or affordable brands on a case-by-case basis, improving adherence and reducing cost burden.
