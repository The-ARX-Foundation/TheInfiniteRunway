### 🖥️ Chromebook Fleet Deployment & Repair

**Situation:** With the onset of COVID-19, the school needed to rapidly deploy ~500 Chromebooks for remote learning.  
**Task:** Prepare, configure, and maintain the entire student Chromebook inventory.  
**Action & Result:**

- Prepared 500+ Chromebooks for emergency COVID-19 deployment by flashing firmware, installing required education software, and enrolling devices in Chrome Device Management (CDM).

- Created clean, sterile hardware environments by deep-cleaning every unit during post-COVID sanitization push, addressing contamination risk and device lifespan.
