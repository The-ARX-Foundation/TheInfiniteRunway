## 🧗‍♂️ **Aggie Climbing Club | Founder & President**

**Texas A&M University | 2021–2024**

---

### 🏛️ **Founding & Organizational Development**

- **Founded and led** Texas A&M’s first officially recognized recreational climbing club, expanding opportunities beyond the competitive team to serve a 400+ student community
    
- **Navigated** complex liability and risk mitigation frameworks by brokering a two-year partnership with the university’s _Outdoor Adventures_ department, aligning club operations under institutional safety standards
    
- **Drafted and formalized** the club’s governing documents, including a multi-section constitution and operations manual detailing protocols for trip planning, community conduct, and liability workflows
    

---

### 🗂️ **Systems & Infrastructure Design**

- **Built** an Airtable-based operations pipeline for managing trip requests, skills assessments, driver clearances, and digital waivers, increasing administrative efficiency and institutional [[Trust]]
    
- **Established** scalable trip-lead structures with a 5:1 participant-to-leader ratio to ensure safety and personal mentorship during outdoor excursions
    

---

### 🤝 **Strategic Partnerships & Financial Accessibility**

- **Negotiated** liability handoffs and group rates with a local indoor climbing gym, reducing membership costs while maintaining high safety standards through third-party oversight
    
- **Secured** resource access and risk coverage through formal university affiliation, enabling legal trip sanctioning and access to funding channels
    

---

### 🧭 **Community Management & Events**

- **Organized** monthly outdoor trips to _Reimers Ranch_, _Medwall_, and other regional climbing sites, fostering skill development and camaraderie among members of all experience levels
    
- **Led** onboarding and community engagement for a 400+ person roster, ensuring inclusivity and consistent group cohesion despite decentralized trip structures
