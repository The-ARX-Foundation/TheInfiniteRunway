### **1. Taylor Webb (Focused Ultrasound Neuromodulation)**

- **Focus:** Developing **ultrasound-based [[Neuromodulation]]** to target deep brain structures for treatment of neurological disorders.
    
- **Angle for you:** Strong translational potential; fits if you’re interested in **noninvasive [[Neuromodulation]]** and clinical trial pipelines.
    

---

### **2. Suva Roy (Vision & Retinal Circuits)**

- **Focus:** **Retinal neural circuitry** in natural vision and diseases like glaucoma, using **viral vectors, optogenetics, electrophysiology, and modeling**.
    
- **Angle for you:** Great fit if you want to dig into **visual BCI foundations** and circuit-level processing tied to perception.
    

---

### **3. Alessandra Angelucci (Visual Cortex Mapping)**

- **Focus:** Mapping **neuronal circuits in the visual cortex** using optical imaging, electrophysiology, and computational modeling.
    
- **Angle for you:** Better suited to **translational vision [[Neuroscience]]**, understanding cortical processing for applications in visual prostheses.
    

---

### **4. Richard Rabbitt (Inner Ear Biophysics & Neural Prosthesis)**

- **Focus:** Biophysics and physiology of the **inner ear**; optical and ultrasound stimulation of hair cells; vestibular injury and repair.
    
- **Angle for you:** Links to **next-gen cochlear/vestibular prostheses**; potential crossover with **neuro-regenerative therapies**.
    

---

### **5. Daniel McDonnall (Ripple Neuro / Peripheral BCI)**

- **Focus:** **Peripheral nerve interfaces** and neuroprosthetics, led **DARPA HAPTIX** for restoring sensation/motor control in prosthetic arms.
    
- **Angle for you:** Perfect for **industry-translation + entrepreneurship**; highly applied, bridging grant-funded research and device commercialization.
    

---

### **6. Tommaso Lenzi (Wearable Robotics & Prosthetics)**

- **Focus:** **Powered prostheses and exoskeletons** to restore or enhance mobility; published in _Science Robotics_ and _Nature Medicine_.
    
- **Angle for you:** Cutting-edge **bionics**; you could explore **accessibility and affordability issues** in prosthetic tech.
    

---

### **7. Jacob George (NeuroRobotics Lab)**

- **Focus:** **Rehabilitation robotics + sensorimotor [[Neural Engineering]]**, with strong emphasis on **translation and commercialization**.
    
- **Angle for you:** Entrepreneurial engineer’s sweet spot; lab actively partners with companies to **deploy clinically viable devices**.
    

---

### **8. Alan Dorval (Neural Information & DBS Optimization)**

- **Focus:** Decoding neuronal information in the basal ganglia; **deep brain stimulation (DBS)** mechanisms, [[Neuromodulation]] design, and regeneration.
    
- **Angle for you:** Good fit for **[[Neuromodulation]] therapies** and [[Computational Neuroscience]]; ties into your interest in **memory, brain-to-brain ideas, and neuro-regeneration**.
    

---

### **9. Christopher Butson (Computational Neuromodulation)**

- **Focus:** Developing neuromodulation therapies using **computational modeling + human studies**; major contributor to DBS Think Tanks.
    
- **Angle for you:** Excellent if you want to learn how **modeling can directly inform clinical neuromodulation**; translational but with a heavy computational bent.

---

### **10. Florian Solzbacher (Neural Interfaces & Biohybrid MEAs)**

- **Focus:** Advanced **neural interface technologies** (Utah Array lineage), **biohybrid transition microelectrode arrays**, smart hydrogels/sensors, and implantable microsystems with an emphasis on **materials, microfabrication, and reliability**.
    
- **Angle for you:** Strong fit if you want to sit **at the materials↔device↔clinical translation junction**. Pairs well with your **entrepreneurial/industry** bent and interest in **BCI/neuromodulation hardware** that can scale from cleanroom to clinic.
