**Dear Hiring Committee,**

I am drawn to MSEI not only for its legacy in developing implantable medical devices, but also for its commitment to innovation in digital health especially in the fields of cardiology and [[Neuromodulation]]. The chance to contribute to technologies that directly improve quality of life for patients with neurological and cardiovascular disorders excites me, and the opportunity to apply data analytics and automation to support both clinical operations and device performance resonates deeply with my career trajectory. I am eager to bring my hybrid expertise in [[Biomedical Engineering]] and software development to your team.

My background gives me a unique perspective on digital health analytics, highlighting not only the necessary competencies to develop functional software, but the medical acummen to understand how it can make a difference in patient outcomes. At Texas A&M University, I worked in Haridas Lab to develop novel cardiac tooling, specifically cardiac intervention prediction algorithms and surgical devices. Here I helped map cardiac surgical data schemas for [[Machine Learning]] readiness, collaborating with clinicians to optimize data collection pipelines. I also led the design of a fetoscopic anchoring tool, from CAD to injection mold fabrication and in-tissue validation, using quantitative imaging to identify and address failure modes.

Similarly, at Gaharwar Lab, I applied rheological studies to model hydrogel behavior, aligning hardware performance with polymer chemistry constraints, and designed a custom low-cost bioprinter to enable experimentation at less than 10 percent of the cost of commercial systems. During my internship at Bhatia Lab at IIT Gandhinagar, I conducted imaging and flow cytometry analysis to evaluate DNA nanocage–drug delivery systems, extracting quantitative insights into therapeutic efficacy. These experiences gave me fluency in advanced statistical analysis and regulatory-compliant biomedical workflows.

Complementing this biomedical foundation, I have developed software tools for intelligent data analysis and automation. For my senior capstone I built JARVIS, a document retrieval system for a major medical device company. JARVIS is powered by Python, LangChain, and PostgreSQL, and achieved sub-millisecond query latency across more than 10,000 engineering documents even when we deployed it on the lowest tier of Azure for scalable replication. Previously in my coursework I also developed Progress, an AI-powered literature analysis pipeline that parsed academic papers and identified research trends using natural language processing and classification models. These projects strengthened my expertise in Python, SQL, cloud deployment, and [[Machine Learning]], aligning with the skills highlighted in this role.

I am eager to bring this hybrid expertise to MSEI, combining my experience in healthcare data interpretation and medical device R&D with my skills in analytics, automation, and backend systems design. I believe this background positions me to contribute meaningfully to your mission of advancing digital health and [[Neuromodulation]] technologies.

Thank you for your time and consideration. I would welcome the opportunity to discuss how my skills and experiences can support MSEI’s mission and to contribute to the next generation of digital health solutions.

**Sincerely,**  
**[[Landon Dahle]]**
