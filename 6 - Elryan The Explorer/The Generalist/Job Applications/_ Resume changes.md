## ✅ **Resume Formatting Checklist**

### 🖋 General Style

-  Keep entire resume **black and white**
    
-  Use **.5 inch margins**
    
-  **Font sizes:**
    
    - Name: 14–16 pt
        
    - Section headings: 12 pt, **ALL CAPS, bold**
        
    - Body text: 11 pt
        

### 🗃 Layout & Structure

-  **Right-align** all dates (not italicized)
    
-  **Right-align** **graduation date only**
    
-  **Bold** organizations/departments
    
-  Titles: _Italicized_, on their own line
    
-  Use **commas**, not pipes (`|`)
    
-  Avoid excessive white space; **fill with concise detail**
    
-  Projects: include **timeframes** and sort in **reverse chronological order**
    
-  **Move Capstone to Projects or Relevant Experience**
    

### ✏️ Content Refinement

-  Change all abbreviations to **full spellings**
    
-  Remove **"Honors"** from GPA section
    
-  Use **present tense** for current experiences
    
-  Bullet points: 1 line if possible
    
    -  Start with **action verb** as far left as possible
        
    -  Format: **what**, **why**, **how** (using X), followed by **impact**
        
        - e.g., “Built __ to automate __, reducing __ by X%”
            
-  Remove filler (a, an, the) unless it improves clarity
    
-  Don't include things they don’t need/care about
    
    - Ask: **“Does this add value or just take up space?”**

## ✅ **Networking Strategy**

### 🧑‍💼 Contact Goals

-  Reach out to **200 people**
    
    -  **5 people per org** you’re interested in
        
    -  **3 people in your target job**
        
    -  **2 higher-ups/managers**
        

### 📬 Outreach Tactics

-  Send **cold emails** or DMs asking for:
    
    -  Industry insights
        
    -  Career advice
        
    -  What they need in the long term
        
    -  Tips or **referrals** if comfortable
        
-  Let them know you want to **learn** from them
    
-  **Research their company & competitors**
    
    -  Figure out **what they do differently**
        
    -  Come prepared to show **how you fit in**
        

### 📘 Resources to Explore

-  AggieNetwork (aggienetworks.com)
    
-  Facebook groups (e.g., [Colorado Biomed Facebook Group](https://www.facebook.com/groups/78461414994/))
    
-  Ask for **referrals** or people to talk to
    
-  Seek out people **already doing the job you want**

## 🧠 **Phase 1: Preparation & Structure**

### ✅ 1. Define the Resume Structure (Sections)

|Order|Section Name|Notes|
|---|---|---|
|1|**Name & Contact Info**|14–16 pt name, city/state, no labels, professional email|
|2|**Objective** _(optional)_|Max 2 lines, e.g., “Seeking full-time role in medical software or [[Neurotech]], starting Fall 2025”|
|3|**Education**|TAMU, full degree name, city/state, grad month/year (right-aligned), GPA 3.71, list minor/certificates|
|4|**Experience**|Work, research, relevant, or volunteer (reverse chrono)|
|5|**Projects**|Capstone, personal, or class projects (include tech/tools)|
|6|**Skills**|Break into “Proficient in” vs “Familiar with”; optionally include future certifications|
|7|**Leadership / Involvement**|Any clubs, teams, orgs|
|8|**Awards / Honors** _(if applicable)_|Omit high school unless exceptional (e.g., Eagle Scout)|
|9|**Coursework** _(optional)_|For early-stage [[Resumes]] or if highly relevant|

## ✏️ Phase 4: Bullet Writing Guidelines

For each bullet:

-  Start with an **action verb** from TAMU list
    
-  Follow **what → how → why → impact**
    
-  Keep bullets **single-line** where possible
    
-  **Quantify** with numbers (#, %, $, X hrs saved)
    
-  Add results: “resulting in...”, “providing...”, “achieving...”
    
-  Avoid abstract adjectives (e.g., “good communicator”) — **show**, don’t tell
    
-  Use the 3-part structure:
    
    > Developed [X system] using [Y method] to [solve Z problem], reducing [bad thing] by [%].
