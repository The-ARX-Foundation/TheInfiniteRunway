# Hopefully Abysmal

Brand identity, website, and digital partnership model for the creative rebellion studio operated by Elryan Dalren.

## Brand Identity

[[Hopefully Abysmal]] has been repositioned from primarily clothing to a full creative studio offering digital partnership services, custom apparel, and open-source knowledge infrastructure.

### Logo

A broken circle with a gap at the bottom-right. When rotated ~28 degrees clockwise, it reads as a comma. Symbolic meaning: "It's okay to pause and reconsider that life is already perfect as it is."

### Design Language

- **Primary palette:** void black (#050505), lime (#C2FF4D), warm white (#F2EFE9)
- **Typography:** DM Serif Display (headings), Space Mono (body/code), Bebas Neue (display)
- **ASCII loading animation** renders the broken circle logo on page load
- **SVG logo** uses stroke-dasharray animation with counter-rotating halos

## Digital Partnership Model

Performance-based compensation. No upfront fees. Join as board advisor, build digital infrastructure, earn commission only on verified incremental revenue.

| Parameter | Value |
|-----------|-------|
| Upfront Cost | $0 |
| Commission Rate | 10-15% on incremental revenue |
| Baseline Period | 90-day pre-partnership revenue average |
| Initial Term | 24 months |
| Exit Clause | Either party, 60-day written notice after month 6 |
| Asset Ownership | Client retains all digital assets at term end |

## Website Structure

- **Hero:** Logo centerpiece with ASCII loader, "Built not to impress. To survive."
- **Mission:** About section with stat row (60 Day Runway, partnerships)
- **Projects:** Live initiatives grid (60 Day Runway, The Vault, SCOOL, Stranded Web)
- **Shop:** Are.na iframe integration for shirt inventory + custom order funnel
- **Partnership:** Full model breakdown with CTA to contact@hopefullyabysmal.com
- **Contact:** Dual routing -- elryan@ for design, contact@ for business
