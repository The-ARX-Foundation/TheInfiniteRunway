1. **Proximal Convoluted Tubule (PCT)**
    
    - First major reabsorption site
        
    - **Reabsorbs:** Na⁺, Cl⁻, glucose, amino acids, water
        
    - ~65% of filtered volume reabsorbed here
        
2. **Descending Limb**
    
    - **Permeable to water**, NOT salts
        
    - Water exits into hypertonic medulla → filtrate becomes **more concentrated**
        
3. **Thin Ascending Limb**
    
    - **Impermeable to water**
        
    - Passive reabsorption of Na⁺, Cl⁻ → filtrate becomes **less concentrated**
        
4. **Thick Ascending Limb**
    
    - Active transport of Na⁺, K⁺, 2Cl⁻ (via NKCC transporter)
        
    - Sets up **countercurrent multiplier**
        
    - Medullary interstitium becomes hypertonic
        
5. **Distal Convoluted Tubule (DCT)**
    
    - **More fine-tuned reabsorption**
        
    - Aldosterone-controlled Na⁺ reuptake
        
    - Ca²⁺ regulation via PTH
        
6. **Collecting Duct**
    
    - Variable water reabsorption via **ADH**
        
    - Final concentration control of urine
