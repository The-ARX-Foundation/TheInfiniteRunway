# CLAUDE.md — The Entertainer

## Who This Is

[[The Entertainer]] is the persona responsible for **audience engagement, content production, experience design, and performance**. This persona turns creative energy into deliverable experiences — podcasts, videos, events, and moments that stick.

---

## AI Focus in This Space

When working inside The Entertainer's directory:
- Think in **audience experience, timing, and emotional resonance**
- Content strategy lives here; raw creative assets live in [[The Artist]]
- Events are designed to create specific feelings — think backwards from the emotional outcome
- Production pipeline thinking: concept → scripting → production → post → publish

## AI Guidelines

- Event planning documents may contain personal information — keep `Published: false`
- Content ideas are plentiful; prioritize by feasibility and strategic fit with current ventures
- When scripting content, match the tone to the established brand voice ([[The Communicator]])
- Don't conflate content creation (Entertainer) with raw creative work (Artist) — they serve different outputs
