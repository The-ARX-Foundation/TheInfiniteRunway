### Sprint 1 - **Day 1-2: Understanding Architecture Fundamentals**

1. **Study the Basics**
    - Learn architectural history: Gothic, Romanesque, Modernist, Postmodern, etc.
    - Understand the role of [[Architecture]] in society, culture, and functionality.
    - **Resources:** _Vitruvius' Ten Books on Architecture_ (classic), _Understanding Architecture_ by Leland Roth, YouTube series on architectural history.

2. **Key Elements of Design**
    - Learn about form, function, scale, proportion, and aesthetics.
    - Study material properties: steel, concrete, wood, glass, composites.
    - Software tools: Download SketchUp or AutoCAD for hands-on exploration.

---

### Sprint 2 - **Day 3-5: Structural Engineering & Building Systems**

1. **Basics of Structural Design**
    - Learn about forces (tension, compression, bending, shear).
    - Understand load-bearing walls, beams, columns, and trusses.
    - Study structural integrity and safety codes.

2. **Building Systems**
    - HVAC, plumbing, and electrical systems in buildings.
    - Passive systems for energy efficiency: natural lighting, ventilation, thermal mass.
    - Sustainability: green roofs, solar panels, recycled materials.
    - **Resources:** _Structures: Or Why Things Don’t Fall Down_ by J.E. Gordon.

---

### Sprint 3 - **Day 6-7: Site Analysis & Urban Planning**

1. **Study Site-Specific Design**
    - Understand how geography, climate, and topography influence [[Architecture]].
    - Explore zoning laws, environmental impact, and building regulations.
    - Analyze famous examples like Fallingwater (site adaptation) or modern skyscrapers.

2. **Urban Planning Basics**
    
    - Learn about designing for communities, transportation, and public spaces.
    - Tools: GIS mapping, site modeling.

---

### Sprint 4 - **Day 8-10: Design Process & Concept Development**

1. **The Design Workflow**
    - Research and ideation: Sketching initial ideas, mood boards.
    - Develop 3D models with free tools like Blender or SketchUp.
    - Prototyping and testing structural feasibility.

2. **Presenting [[Architecture]]**
    - Learn to create blueprints and floor plans (AutoCAD basics).
    - Practice rendering and presentation graphics (Lumion, Photoshop).

---

### Sprint 5 - **Day 11-12: Case Studies of Great Architects**

1. **Study Masterpieces**
    - Analyze famous works: Frank Lloyd Wright, Le Corbusier, Zaha Hadid, I.M. Pei.
    - Identify the innovation and context behind these designs.
    - Make an Are.na board for it

2. **Critically Evaluate**
    - What works in these designs? What can be improved?
    - Try replicating small elements in your own design to learn.

---

### Sprint 6 - **Day 13-14: Rapid Prototyping & Iterative Design**

1. **Build Your First Project**
    - Sketch or digitally create your architectural vision.
    - Apply structural principles and site considerations.

2. **Seek Feedback**
    - Share your design with peers, mentors, or online communities for critique.
    - Iterate based on feedback: refine aesthetics, improve functionality.

---

### Sprint 7 - **Day 15: Final Presentation**

1. **Complete Your Design Portfolio**
    - Include sketches, blueprints, renders, and a write-up on your process.
    - Summarize lessons learned from the 15-day journey.

2. **Simulate Licensing Exam Basics**
    - Review study materials for the Architectural Registration Exam (ARE).
    - Practice questions on building codes, professional practice, and project delivery.

---

### **Additional Tips**

- **Time Management:** Dedicate focused hours daily to avoid distractions.
- **Networking:** Join [[Architecture]] forums or communities for support (e.g., Reddit r/architecture).
- **Document Everything:** Capture sketches, reflections, and learnings—these will be invaluable later.

By staying committed to this structured approach, you’ll gain a solid foundation in architecture and create something you’re proud to share.
