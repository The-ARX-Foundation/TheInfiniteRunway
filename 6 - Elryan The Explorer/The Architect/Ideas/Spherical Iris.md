Motorized spherical iris for roof that can open up.

Rotation on plane tangential to circular base, bars / triangular pieces fitting sphere shape attached to motors.

Enter from underground; stairs on each side s.t. lecture hall style works; stairs with chairs and stage down at middle on entrance side.

Projectors that are linked s.t. display program can be made to project one image onto ceiling, aka stars / weather akin to the vegas sphere.

Use resonators / noise blocking techniques so the motors are silent save a few key frequencies s.t. a chord is produced when utilizing the motors.
