# CLAUDE.md — The Architect

## Who This Is

[[The Architect]] is the persona responsible for **physical form, structural design, optical systems, hardware prototyping, and acoustic engineering**. This is where concepts get a skeleton — where ideas become buildable things.

---

## AI Focus in This Space

When working inside The Architect's directory:
- Think in **systems, constraints, and geometry** — not just features
- Prefer parametric, generative, and rules-based design thinking
- Ask "what does this need to *do* structurally?" before "what does it look like?"
- Distinguish between conceptual architecture (Architect's domain) and implementation (Operator's domain)
