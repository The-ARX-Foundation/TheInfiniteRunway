# The Architect

The Architect is the persona responsible for **structural design, product engineering, and optical systems research**. This covers physical [[Architecture]], hardware product design, and the extensive camera/lens optics laboratory.

---

## Scope

- Residential and commercial [[Architecture]] ([[Parametric Housing]], acoustic design)
- Hardware product design and prototyping
- Optical systems and camera technology R&D
- Industrial design [[Philosophy]] and methodology
- Physical computing and sensor integration

## Key Projects

### Optics Lab
A concentrated collection of camera, lens, and imaging technology ideas spanning telecentric lenses, light-field cameras, plenoptic imaging, catadioptric systems, DIY lens fabrication, and experimental modalities (sound-as-camera, WiFi imaging). These represent a coherent R&D thread in novel imaging hardware.

### Product Design Ideas
- [[Parametric Residential Housing Blueprints]]
- Helmholtz Resonator Bricks (acoustic [[Architecture]])
- [[Spherical Iris]] (architectural feature)
- [[Midi Bagpipes]] (electronic instrument hardware)
- [[Foldable Pillow]] / [[Laptop Modifications]] (consumer products)
