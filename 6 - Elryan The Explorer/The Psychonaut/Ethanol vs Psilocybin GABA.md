## 🜗 Comparative Alchemy: Ethanol vs. Psilocybin

### ✴️ I. Core Mechanism

| Substance                     | Primary Receptor Target                 | Net Effect on Neural Activity                                    |
| ----------------------------- | --------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Alcohol (Ethanol)**         | GABA-A (↑), NMDA (↓)                    | Global inhibition → *Disinhibition*, sedation, [[Euphoria]]          |
| **Psilocybin (via Psilocin)** | 5HT2A (↑), 5HT1A (↑), GABA (modulatory) | Network destabilization → *Entropy*, ego dissolution, plasticity |

---

### ✴️ II. Role of GABA

| Aspect                 | **Alcohol**                                                                                     | **Psilocybin**                                                                      |
| ---------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Direct GABA Action** | Positive allosteric modulator of **GABA-A** receptors → Cl⁻ influx → neuronal hyperpolarization | **Indirect** stimulation of GABA release via **5HT2A** on interneurons              |
| **GABA-B** involvement | Minor but present; implicated in reward circuitry                                               | Less characterized; psilocin may affect tone indirectly                             |
| **Chronic Impact**     | Alters subunit expression (e.g., ↑α4/δ) → tolerance, withdrawal                                 | May increase GABA activity temporarily, as a *counterbalance* to 5HT2A excitability |

> 🜍 *Conclusion*: While ethanol **enhances GABAergic tone** directly (sedation), psilocybin **engages GABA via serotonergic modulation**, particularly **inhibitory interneurons**. It does not bind GABA receptors itself but can still sway their power.

## 🜂 Unified Insight: GABA's Involvement

* GABA is a **convergence point**, but its role is vastly different:

  * **In Alcohol**, GABA is the **conduit**—the **main actor**.
  * **In Psilocybin**, GABA is a **reactive element**, shaped by **serotonergic dynamics** and feedback regulation.
