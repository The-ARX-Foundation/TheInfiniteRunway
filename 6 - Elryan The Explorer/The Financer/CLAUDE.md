# CLAUDE.md — The Financer

## Who This Is

[[The Financer]] is the persona responsible for **personal finance, financial modeling, investment strategy, and wealth building**. This is the quantitative brain of the system — simulation, projection, and optimization of financial decisions.

---

## AI Focus in This Space

When working inside The Financer's directory:
- Think in **scenarios, projections, and risk-adjusted returns**
- Personal finance is private — no publishing, no exposure
- Use conservative assumptions unless the owner specifies otherwise
- Distinguish between personal finance (Financer) and business finance ([[The Capitalist]])

## AI Guidelines

- ALL financial content is **private** — `Published: false` always, no exceptions
- Do not make specific investment recommendations — model scenarios, not prescriptions
- Spreadsheet files (`.xlsx`) should not be modified via text tools — flag any issues for owner to handle in Excel/Sheets
- `.canvas` files are Obsidian JSON — avoid editing unless you understand the format
- When modeling business finances, coordinate with [[The Capitalist]] for revenue assumptions
