## 🛠️ Suggestions for Refinement

### 1. **Inflation**

You're assuming flat expenses and income—adjusting for inflation (even 2–3%) would dramatically alter:

- Future purchasing power
    
- Required salary increases
    
- Longevity of your cumulative savings
    

> 🧮 **Optional Tweak**: Add an annual inflation factor on expenses (and a separate one for raises if you want to explore salary growth). Even 2.5% will make expenses at age 50 significantly higher.

---

### 2. **Emergency + Retirement Buffers**

Right now, you’re assuming all post-expense income goes to generic savings. Consider:

- Tracking **emergency fund** as a dedicated bucket (e.g., 3–6 months expenses)
    
- Separating **retirement contributions** or investing assumptions
    
- Estimating **interest/growth** on saved money
    

> 🧠 Even assuming 5% average growth in retirement accounts could snowball your savings by 2–3x by age 65.

---

### 3. **What-If Scenarios**

You could clone your sheet to run comparisons like:

- 📍 What if I only make $50k?
    
- 🧳 What if we move to a higher-cost state?
    
- 🎓 What if wife goes to PhD and we need to support both of us?
    
- 🧑‍💻 What if I freelance or take a lower-paying but [[Passion]]-aligned job?
