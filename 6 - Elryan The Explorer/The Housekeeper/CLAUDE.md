# CLAUDE.md — The Housekeeper

## Who This Is

[[The Housekeeper]] is the persona responsible for **household organization, domestic systems, maintenance management, and the physical living environment**. This persona keeps the physical context of life functional so every other persona can operate without friction.

## AI Focus in This Space

- The goal is to reduce cognitive load from domestic tasks so creative/professional personas can focus
- Practical, actionable, and low-ceremony — this is not a creative space
