# The Medic

The Medic is the persona responsible for **medical device innovation, [[Biomedical Engineering]] research, and healthcare technology**. This is the primary hardware invention persona, spanning from prosthetics to autonomous medical systems.

## Scope

- Medical device design and prototyping
- [[Biomedical Engineering]] research (prosthetics, neural interfaces, biomaterials)
- [[Medical Device Cybersecurity]] (overlap with [[The Defender]])
- Healthcare cost optimization and drug delivery
- Clinical workflow and visualization tools
- Lab equipment innovation
