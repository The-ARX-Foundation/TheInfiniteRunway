Themes: [[Biomedical Engineering]]

Record brainwaves of patients while doing tasks with mocap; relate the mocap to motor / hydraulic compression values to move a prosthesis in same fashion of movement with relation to brainwaves via ML over many patients.

Hypothesis: similarities in brainwaves would cary training over to amputees with advanced prosthetics; with brainwave measurement we can replicate movement in prosthetics with AI.

  

Training feedback:

Initial Mocap

Brain activity during mocap

Superimposed model of prosthetic over mocap

Once virtual testing is done, transfer to real model

Mocap the model on a dummy; move my body and it should mimic my arm movement perfectly; mocap both and have it calculate loss through difference in mocaps (offset base of prosthetic in case my torso moves)

Built in gyroscope for prosthetic?

Is movement of a limb different in brain when in different orientations / gravitational settings?

Muscle / electrode sock; rubber sheath with hundreds of electrodes to give local tracking and tailor per patient

Tattoos for rotation of sock

Secondary algorithm that determines degree of rotation of each ring from initial conditions to set offset to bring closest to OG training position; allows for data to stay consistent.

Calibration via set movements / instructions for patient
