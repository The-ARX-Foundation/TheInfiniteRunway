# Welcome to the vault of Hopefully Abysmal

This is a **living** archive of experiments, frameworks, and survival strategies by Landon Dahle // Elryan the Explorer. Dive in, explore the edge of chaos, and remember: **creation is salvation.**

**Legal:** by continuing, you accept the [license](./license.md) in this repository (The ARX Foundation Public Pact, AFPP) — tldr: do not get in the way. This is the **Infinite Runway** chapter: the work is **indefinite**, public, and **ongoing** (The 60 Day Runway was the **opening sprint** in the same story, archived [here](https://github.com/Hopefully-Abysmal/The60DayRunway)).
