[PUPARIA](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CWnqX41JHuM) by [Shingo Tamagawa](https://www.youtube.com/@shingotamagawa4740) is an independantly created animation that is absolutely stunning. The ultrastylized and evocative artstyle utilized completely individualizes the work from others of similar feeling. Though no dialogue is provided, the work speaks to the viewer through the characters [[Emotions]], a sense of simultaneous [[Longing]], [[Fear]], and awe; a world to imagine. A must watch when in need of [[Inspiration]]
