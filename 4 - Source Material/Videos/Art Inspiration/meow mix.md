[meow mix](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GiaD7WSdb4M) by [meowballz](https://www.youtube.com/@meowballz1) (a niche internet microcelebrity that popped up on my recommendations by the blessing of the YouTube Algorithm Gods) genuinely deserves some credit; somehow it perfectly captures a snapshot of internet culture from the 2000s onwards, a prime example of "art that could only exist because of the internet" - @mallbat on YouTube. By utilizing a mixture of media sources, styles, and references it encapsulates the essence of an era curated by the masses; the culture of the internet as a whole condensed into a sub 5 minute mental barrage of color and creativity.  
As I browse the comment section I find a vast majority of those willing to give their thoughts and opinions share my viewpoint, that of [[Nostalgia]] mixed with a hint of [[Fear]] and awe of the dream incarnate we had all just witnessed. Some feel [[Sadness]] for their ability (or lack thereof) to comprehend, while others marvel at the technical expertise and the perfectly flawed execution of the visuals alone: "if you blink you miss 3 hours of editing" - @ShepDance on YouTube. "buh" - @VivetMuri on YouTube perfectly sums up the collective feeling of speechlessness induced by this lobotomy of a creation; far greater than the content itself this compilation strikes true to its target audience, the chronically online generation, [[The Human]] side of the internet, those "born too late to explore the world, born too early to explore the universe, born just in time to experience meow mix" - @whitericeallday on YouTube. 
Overall the best video I've seen in a while, thank you.
