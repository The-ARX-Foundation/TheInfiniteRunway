4:45
"The friction of the devices we use is dictated by the number of inputs and the time to output the information we require." 
Takes away from our interactions with real life, the more time spent on technology is directly equivalent to the less time we spend in our surroundings. The reduction of inputs and processing time is aimed at reducing friction.

5:30
"Ambient computing" - by constantly processing the information around you, the inputs can be reduced to almost zero. EX Google's "now playing" and Microsoft's "Recall" features. Though I am initially thrown off by their invasion of privacy, that privacy is in reality already an illusion. Might as well lean into them and reap the benefits.
