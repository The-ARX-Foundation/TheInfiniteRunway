# Alex Hormozi - 13 Years Of Brutally Honest Business Advice in 90 Mins

- Start selling to rich then make your way down to poor;
    - Volume based strategy; “solve rich people problems for rich people prices”
    - Expectations based on percentage of total wealth.
    - Rich people pay for time above all else
        - Reputation > Guarantee
- Know the problem you are actually trying to solve
    - Prioritization of resources; time, money, people, etc.
        - Have one clear goal for one clear company for one clear customer.
    - Define the problem you are trying to solve.
    - The business as a whole has to be balanced; have ideal ratios for everything, not just on what interests you the most / your strengths. The business will only be as strong as its weakest link.
        - Businesses grow to the constraint of the founder; then it is limited to what you dont like. Learn it or hire it on.
            - Hiring tips:
                - Interview a ton of people. (EX. hundreds)
                    - Interview starter round, ask them for the best people they know, continue until you find someone who you now can make the best judgement on.
                - Act as if you don’t know things
                    - “How does what you do make the business money?”
                        - Moving bottom line more than it pays for them.
                        
                    - “Look for quantity and quality of metrics they use to describe their role.”
                        - How do they do their job and explain it to you?
