Attendees: Dr. Haridas, Landon

Objective: Introduction to Project

Notes

* Problem: Chorioamniotic Membrane Delamination  
* Origin: Box stitch without c-section  
* Gen 1: ChorioAnchor as described in current literature  
  * Diam \~1.2 mm (driven by needle size, 14g)  
  * Multi-stage Injection (non-ideal)  
  * PLG8218  
    * Degrades over known and desired timeframe  
* Gen 2 Process:   
1. Injection Mold  
2. Set secondary shape (shape memory polymer, still PLG8218)  
   1. Increase temperature above Tg  
   2. Squeeze rapidly  
   3. Immediately cool (quench) to below Tg  
3. Pre-set in needles  
   1. Ideally utilizing 16 gauge needle for injection  
4. Inject  
5. Revert to the initial shape  
   1. Rapidly increase temperature above Tg  
      1. Resistive heating  
         1. Mg Core (still degradable)  
         2. Mg particles s.t. entire staple is conductive  
   2. Wait until reset (time TBD)  
   3. Cool to solidify to the point of rigidity required for anchoring  
6. Anchor
