### Dell SCM Intro
BIG company, hundreds of billions in revenue.
	Small margins, supply chain >75 billion
Big on recycling, prevents 2 billion pounds of e-waste per year

OG business model was highly customizable, but became a problem
	Switched to good, better, best model
		Allows 3rd party manufacturing in mass
Manufacturing in house for countries with high tariffs

Tracking of parts via PPID
	Tracking of sources of parts is difficult, but they try.
	UFPLA - Lithium slave labor
		Dell buys batteries, they are still responsible if their battery suppliers are being unethical
	Know the suppliers suppliers so you can connect people if disruptions occur

Disruptions need plans:
	EX: Ukraine vs Russia
		Scan through customer records to find people on list they couldn't work with
			Couldn't just stop working in Russia due to hospitals (and greed lmao)
	EX: Tornados in Nashville TN
		Both facilities in close proximity
			1. Ensure nobody was injured
			2. Customer orders?
				Contact Customers and rank priority
			3. Get operations up
				Storage, Distribution, Manufacturing
				Needs focus, put resources into one thing at a time
