# Rapid Design and Prototyping
Key word **RAPID**
	Design digitally, make changes often, iterate on one property at a time.

Scale up once finalized

Design Objectives / milestones (in the order listed)
	Proof of Concept (POC) - prove that it can be built / function to meet the goals of the project
	Visual Prototype - Look and feel
	Working Prototype - full features
	Engineering Prototype - used for design for manufacturing (determining QC steps)
