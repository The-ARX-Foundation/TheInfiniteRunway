## Sales vs. Marketing
Marketing: To make people aware
	Info dump - Branding
		Promotion of the product / idea; distinctive designs and trademarks
			EXPENSIVE (see pets.com)
			Deere started with 25% of operating budget, now has decreased to 5%
			Target end user AND/OR paying customer
				ex. intel inside to general public but paying customer is computer manufacturer
					Selling sales
				What is important to your customer base?
					Cost
					Quality
					Extrinsic Value
						features / what is gained
					Trustworthiness
						Standing behind product?
				How do you convert awareness to a sale? (Marketing to Sales Information)
					Limited time offer (FOMO)
					Build a relationship
					Demonstrate your product / service 
						Free trial type beat
						Freemium model
					Prove performance with data
			Determine target demographic / sales channels
				Location?
				Habits/Interests?
			Tools for reaching your customer base
				SEO
					Websites
				[[Social Media]]
					Influencers
				Print ads
					Trade Publications / Magazines
				Video ads
				Email/Text campaigns
				Trade shows / Conferences
				Free Trials
			Customer types:
				Prospective
				Early Adopter
				New Customer
				... (review slideshow)
				Manager vs final decision maker (boss / stakeholder) vs end user
	Sends leads to sales

Sales: Focus on making the transaction happen
	1 on 1 with the customer; feedback from customers is key
		Sends feedback to marketing to better market
	Customer objections (sales to marketing information)
		Price vs value
			Missing key features
				Cost of implementation vs. market acquired (potential profit)
			Switching costs
				Perception (ex. was pushing for competition and doesn't want to switch up)
		Incompatibility with current workflow
		Not well known / view against competition
	Types of Sales: (determines marketing and sales strategy)
		B2B - Low volume high margin
			Supplies
			Distribution (ex. selling to Walmart)
			Service (ex. consultancy)
		B2C - high volume low margin; less on relationship more on sale
			Seller to consumer
		Direct Sales
			Manufacturer to consumer, no 3rd party
		SAAS
		Enterprise
			Complex over longer period of time; might include product and contract for additional services
			Corporate to corporate
				Development of some sort
	Pricing strategies
		Set price to whatever the customer is willing to pay in normal conditions
		Look at competitors
			List price vs price paid can differ
			If you provide additional value, add it!
		Similar products can compete on:
			Price (dont do this first; lowers your value)
			Features
				Value gained
				UVP
			Quality
				longer lasting, better performing
			Convenience
				lower lead times
				easier to use
			Customer Experience / Service
				Better vibes
			Brand / Reputation
				

Common goal: increase revenue ***and net profits***
