Course: ENGR 312

1.  Who was your favorite guest speaker in the course and why?
I would say that Mr. Ben Collinsworth was my favorite guest speaker in the course (possibly due to relevance bias as he was the last to speak). Not only was his lecture super informative, he and I had a great conversation after class and in the 489 class as well.
2.  What was your favorite case study in this course and why?
I really enjoyed the Toyota case study, it provided insights into how even insanely innovative companies can fumble, a guide to some pitfalls to consider.
3.  What was your favorite presentation assignment in this course and why?
I really liked the supply chain presentation assignment; let me do a deep dive into a hypothetical situation in a new way that I had never done before.
4.  What was your favorite lecture in this course and why?
My favorite lecture from the course would have to be the one on Manufacturing (10-22-2024, lecture 16 in my notes but might not be accurate). I'm a big product development guy so learning more about the field was great.
5.  What are you taking away from this course?
Overall a new perspective on how to run a company! I really valued having your insight throughout the semester; being able to learn from someone who's been through the thick of it and is able to clearly articulate how they got through it has been immensely beneficial.
