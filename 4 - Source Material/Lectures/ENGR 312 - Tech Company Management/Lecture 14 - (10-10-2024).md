## Sourcing and Procurement
Procurement:

Sourcing:
	Information Gathering:
		RFI - Request for Information
		RFQ - Request for Quote
	Vendor Scoring in ERP
		Quantity scrapped
		Quality testing
		Etc.

Purchasing:
	Negotiating [[Contracts]]

Documents:
	PO - Purchase Order
		Agreed upon Terms (Legally binding)
			Amount to be paid
			Payment Method
			Schedule
				Net 30
					30 days
				3/10 Net 30
					3% discount if paid in 10 days, normal from 11-30
			Late Payment Penalty
				5% per day or max allowed by law
				typically irrelevant until it gets to cost to take to court
		Often signed by both parties (if efficiency is an issue can make exceptions, but this is previously agreed upon)
		Shipping Terms:
			FOB - Free On Board
			FOB Destination
				Supplier pays for shipping
					Supplier responsible until delivered
			FOB Origin / FOB Shipping Port
				Once off dock all responsibility transferred
