## Manufacturing process

Centrifugal Casting

Die Castings
	Molten Metal under high pressure

Permanent Mold Castings
	Gravity / vacuum fed
	Cheaper to set up but less precise compared to die casting
	Can do ferrous material!
	Clamps

Sand Casting
	Use 3d print to make indent and then fill with material

Molding
	Plastics (like die casting, but not metal)
	Types:
		Injection
		Blow
			e.g. water bottles
		Extrusion
			e.g. PVC pipe
		Rotational
			e.g. kayaks, lil tikes car and slide
		Thermoforming
			stamping but plastic
			e.g. Tupperware
		Powder Metallurgy
			Sintering; upgrade to casting (less power and material, higher purity, ability to mix metals)
			e.g. a lot of car parts / fine mechanics
			SLS

Forming

Forging

Bending
	Extruded sheet into bending press

Stamping
	Hydraulic press of metal sheet

Shearing
	Cuts strips

Machining - Subtractive Manufacturing
	Sub-types
		CNC
		Milling

Joining
	Welding - melt together
	Soldering - third material
	Brazing - soldering but all 3 are fusing together
	Adhesive Bonding - typically for non-metallic substances
		New: Diffusion bonding of metals
	Mechanical Assembly - nuts, bolts, screws

Additive manufacturing
	3d printing
