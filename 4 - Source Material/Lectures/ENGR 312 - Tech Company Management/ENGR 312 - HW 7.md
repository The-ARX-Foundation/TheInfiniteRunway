## 1. What is your take away from our in class discussion on Toyota and their relationship with ChassisCo?  What would you do if your were in Toyota's position?  What would you do if you were in ChassisCo's position?

My biggest take away from the in class discussion on the Toyota & ChassisCo relationship was that they honestly both dropped the ball. ChassisCo was obviously not prepared to handle the new requirements in addition to going solo, and while they should have communicated that from the get-go Toyota should not have acted as they did throughout the process. In Toyota's position I would have likely not pulled all staff in the first place until they demonstrated they were chill with the new requirements. In ChassisCo's position I would have asked for help later on, yes its emberrassing but you know what you can and can't do; don't over promise and under deliver.  

## 2.  What manufacturing processes discussed in class was the most exciting to you?  Is there a manufacturing process that you would like to know more about but we did not discuss in class?

I [[Love]] 3d printing, I'm super curious how it could be utilized en-mass for personalized products on a commercial scale. Outside of additive manufacturing, I am intrigued by E-textiles; I feel if we were to make the MyoSense a real product they would be pivotal.

## 3.  What is your big takeaway from guest speaker Ben Collinsworth?  What could Mr. Collinsworth do to improve his talk?  Would you recommend him as a speaker for this class next semester?

My biggest takeaway from Mr. Ben Collinsworth's lecture was that innovation can occur anywhere; commercial landscaping was not really even on my radar but it was super compelling. Additionally, his view on automation and outlook for how we will be utilizing AI and [[Machine Learning]] mimicked my own and we were able to have a really engaging conversation after class that was super intriguing. I do feel that the delivery of the different technologies was a little confusing, maybe go through a timeline first then discuss alternatives and why they don't mess with them after. I would most definitely recommend him as a speaker for the class next semester; genuinely a very inspiring speaker and a networking [[Connection]] I most definitely would not have made otherwise.
