## Sales Channels
Wholesale = Distributer, Producer = Manufacturer

Indirect sales:
	Pros:
		Larger customer outreach
			Can help with international relations
				Try to find someone with experience in business both here and there though
		Value Added Resellers (VAR) can help out your branding a ton
			You are Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM)
	Cons:
		Middlemen, continually adding to final cost
			Margin = Gross Profit/Revenue = (Price-Cost)/Price
				Impossible to be >1 (percentages)
			Markup Multiplier = 1/(1-Margin) = Price/Cost
				Often confused with margins
		They may demand exclusivity
			They might fall through
				HAVE A TIMEFRAME
				HAVE CLAUSES
		Lose control of your branding
			know their reputation, your product depends on it

Leasing model:
	Good for products with high resale values
	EX:
		MRI's
		Airplanes
			GM acted as turbine seller then bought the airplanes that they made to lease (multi-faceted business model is >>>)
		Construction Equipment
	Pros:
		Lowers upfront cost to leasee so you can charge a premium
			Opens up market to people without ability to purchase up front
				Can have clauses to decrease price as they grow to purchase if they want
		Leasing [[Contracts]] can have clauses demanding insurance on the product to mitigate risk
			if broken / lost their insurance covers cost (can be more than sale price even)
			can have clause requiring you are the person who does the maintenance 
		Can even apply depreciation to taxes!
			Is still an investment / asset, not an expense
	Cons:
		High upfront costs
			Partner with another company if need be
				Potentially a company that was trying to buy you out
		High risk of not getting paid
			If company goes out of business you're stuck with the product potentially without enough to cover the equipment (now used too)

Private Labeling
	Manufacturers who dont want to brand their products
		typically retailer specific with exclusivity
			EX: Kirkland, HEB, MORPHE (makeup brand)
White Labeling
	Product manufactured for multiple retailers
		retailer doesn't have a say on the product
			EX: Amazon basics, some HEB as well (salsa jar vs plastic container example)
