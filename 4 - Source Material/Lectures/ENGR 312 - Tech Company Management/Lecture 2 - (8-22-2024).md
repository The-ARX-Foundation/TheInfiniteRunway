Primary Aspects of a company
	Produces Product (service, widget, etc.) therefore must have the operational capabilities to produce and sell the product.

*From the perspective of an existing company*
	Organization via hierarchy; top level chart as follows:
		Chief Executive Officer (CEO) >
		Chief Financial Officer (CFO) > CRO > Compliance
		Chief Operating Officer (COO) > Manufacturing > SCM > Engineering > Regional Offices
		General Council (Legal)
		Chief Administrative Officer (CAO) > HR > Comm.
		Chief Commercialization Officer (CCO) > Sales > Marketing > Branding
		Business Development
		IT/IS
		Chief Technology Officer (CTO) > R&D
	

Concept of '[[Death]] by Attorney' is comedic but a real concern.
