Guest Lecture - Taha Raja with FMW Fasteners

FMW - Fasteners for Metal and Wood (also "Feed My Wife")

Bought the business from local provider, warehouse was "a nightmare"
	Was looking for a business in the industrial space
		"Fractured industry"
	When considering this, make sure you include the cost of sustaining your family
		The first day will not be enough to pay this, don't be discouraged but ensure you have a plan for this.

Started during financial crisis
	Housing crisis

COVID
	Air freight cost less than container shipping for a while

Implemented Oracle Netsuite ERP
	To implement an ERP, first create a requirements document; what do you want to automate?, what could the system do for you?
	Folks were resistant to change
		Especially with legacy companies
		Must ensure they know it wont replace them, rather give them more time to do other things
			Extracting more productivity from your labor force

Bought new warehouse
	Organized by velocity (what's selling)
		Cycle count inventory
			Figures out actual count via possibility of mess ups
				"System says there are # , double check"
				Whenever a human comes into contact with something, the chance of a screw up occurs
	Now completely e-commerce
		System picks between warehouses and dropshipping
	Waves:
		Organized by aisle
			picker gets a wave and picks parts per aisle
		Totes split into orders by packaging
			packaging picked by system by weight
				customer gets to pick when they want it, system picks logistical method
	

Fired unprofitable customers
	cost sold - cost to sell != margin
		Think of complex stuff:
			Pay schedule
				Improper cash flow, net 120 vs supplier net 30
			Labor
			Cost to re-package

You have to experiment, compare, and implement new solutions
	E-commerce experimentation
		Mobile is key nowadays
		Optimize speed to checkout

Monitor your competitive landscape
	Regardless of how exciting your landscape is, there will be competition and thus innovation

Internal Obstacles:
	Your own security = start of downfall lmao
		Stay vigilant; how can you do better?
	Audit yourself
		let other people into your business and tell you what you're doing wrong
External Obstacles:
	UPS Strikes!!
		Customer doesn't care; have backups
		Analyze failure rates with specifics to locations
		Split between carriers

Set your goals wisely:
	Not to have big warehouse; rather to deliver on time. That is the goal so change your KPIs and OKRs respectively

Seamless communication can be achieved by inter-mingling teams in analysis
	i.e. warehouse team members on business committee

Offer your product / service for money, not free
	Customers will ask for things, you may feel obligated to do it but you need to think about how it affects your business
		Bottom line, if its not profitable dont do it
	A customer is a business relationship

Get some experience then start consulting companies lol
	Implemented ERP, share knowhow for a price

Travel Co. - Brambleberry Tours
	Photography / safari in africa
		Said to hit him up to get hooked up for a safari tour

Customer breakdown?
	20% account for 80% of revenue (b2b)
	b2c serves as testing ground for new stuff though; also launchpads concepts to b2b (intermingling)

Emerging Technology
	Automated pickers
		Hard with bulk

Tariffs?
	Increases cost, just has to be passed down
	Due to it being a commodity item, people just buy it
		Some opportunity for substitution
	Moved from China to Taiwan, Vietnam, and India
		Hardly any manufacturers in US nowadays
