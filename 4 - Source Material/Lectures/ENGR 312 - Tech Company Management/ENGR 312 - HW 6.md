## 1.  Refer back to Case Study #2 on the supply chain.  Do you think the COVID-19 crisis demonstrated the resilience of the global supply chain or the weakness of the global supply chain?

I honestly think it demonstrated both the resilience and weakness of the global supply chain. The weaknesses exposed include the dependency on single sources and geographic concentration, limitations in just-in-time (JIT) inventory models, logistical bottlenecks and labor shortages, and limited digital transformation across supply chains. However, many strengths arose such as rapid innovation and a shift to domestic production, accelerated digital transformation and technology adoption, redundant sourcing and regionalization, and an increased focus on sustainability and risk management. There is a lot to work on, but now we have the supply chain in focus to actually work on it.
