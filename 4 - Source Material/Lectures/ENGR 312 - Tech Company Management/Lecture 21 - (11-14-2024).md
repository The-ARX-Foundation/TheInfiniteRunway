# Quality
Building to uniformity > building to spec
	Longevity
	Continuous improvement needed

Quality circles
	Quality board with someone from all aspects of the process (not just the machinists)
		Discussing  how quality can get better 

'Quality is Free' - Philip Crsoby 1979
	"If you don't have time to do it right, then when will you have time to do it over?"
	Aim for zero defects
		"every process can be made better"

Gap Framework
	1. Gap between what management believes the customer wants and what the customer actually wants
	2. Gap between individual goals and company goals
	3. Gap between written process and the actual process
	4. Gap between what the company promised the customer and what is actually delivered
	5. Gap between customers expectations and the customer experience

Pareto Analysis
	Figure out why things come in for repairs and chart with a pareto chart
		Focus on far left of the chart where quantity is sorted from most to least from left to right

Six Sigma
	Goal: no more than 3.4 defects per million
	DMAIC
		Define
		Measure
		Analyze
		Improve (The heart of Six Sigma)
		Control

Deere Strategies
	Know the voice of the customer
		Collect Data
			Customer surveys/feedback
		Analyze Data
		Act Quickly
			Relevance of data goes by quick
	Measure the costs of quality
		Cost of prevention
			Training, implementation, etc.
		Cost of Appraisals
			Testing, meetings, etc.
		Cost of Internal Failures 
			Pre-customer
			Scrap cost
		Cost of External Failures
			Discovered after delivery to customer or worse by the customer
			Recall / repair costs
			Goodwill costs
