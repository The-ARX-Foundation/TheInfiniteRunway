## Market Research:
Premise 1 - hired to be technical sales person
	* Need to do product research
	* Need to figure out what the company can do (customizations)
Premise 2 - new product in existing company
	* Determine sales channels
		* Marketing materials, messaging, etc.
	* Determine what problems it solves; what are the pain points?
		* How does your product compare to competition?
			* Price
			* Pain Points
			* TAM (is it more general or specific?)

[[Market Research]] Strives to comprehend:
	Potential customers
		value?
	Market trends
	Barriers to entry
		Price
		Shifting factors
	Competition
	Risks
		potential damage to customer
		economic risk (lack of sales surety)
	Environmental Factors
	Financials
		ROI

Why?
	Needed to introduce new product
	Needed to re-introduce failing product (rebranding)
	"Nobody wants to spend their time on something that fails :/"
		Obtain corporate buy in
		Attract Key personnel

Key Questions:
	Who is my customer?
	Who is my end user?
	Who are my competitors?
		How do customers feel about competitors?
	What do customers want?
	What price can you charge?
	How should you approach the market?
		What is the message
		

Misc.
	Have technical salespeople test the product; they need to be able to help the customers if they run into issues.
