## Misc Tips
Don't give up too much equity too early
	Sweat equity is real though, use it.
		Over vesting period
[[Trust]] in your gut/vision
	Its ultimately up to you to design what is best for the company
	Advisors are good, but you are there for a reason.
Getting the most out of the resources you have available
	Check out the business school!
Overuse the word "Partner"
	You want to make everything a partnership, symbiotic relationship
Talk about the company with as many people as possible.
	Once you have IP protected but yeah
	People want to network
		People will have their own connections to put you with
		Take rejection with an ask for people to talk to
Build a strong advisory board
	Share your vision
Be observant
	Understand peoples needs = sale
	Tailor your pitch
		Ex: left handed pen that smears (wolf of wall street "sell me this pen")
		Ex: theft and lost product for cannabis
		Ex: tax revenue for auditing / state

**Break down your goals into the steps you must reach to get there**

Build a blog!
	SEO
	Reporting things in real time for investors
	Helps bring in customers, early adopters, and build a community
		Potential collaboration opportunities
			Blog for blog relationships
