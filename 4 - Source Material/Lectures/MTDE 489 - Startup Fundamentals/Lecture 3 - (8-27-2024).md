## After POC?
Dont forget about MVP, keep working at it

Prototyping
	Build iteratively
		Focus on small selection of pieces at a time
	Set a plan with MILESTONES
		Do things in parallel if you can
	Get customer feedback AS YOU GO
		Again, see that MVP and keep it updated

Types of Prototypes: (Co-pilot cause I missed the slide)
1. **Sketches and Diagrams**: Simple visual representations on paper.
2. **3D Printing**: Physical models created using 3D printers.
3. **Feasibility Prototypes**: Test technical possibilities.
4. **Horizontal Prototypes**: Focus on user interface.
5. **Vertical Prototypes**: Focus on backend and database design.
6. **Role-Playing**: Use virtual or augmented reality for visualization.
7. **Video Prototypes**: Animated presentations of concepts.
8. **Working Models**: Functional versions to test usability.
9. **Physical Models**: Scaled-down versions of the final product.
10. **Wireframes**: Digital diagrams for website layouts

Why prototype? (Cause its fun and why not lmao)
	Increases value of your idea; without it its almost worthless (and probably bad)
	Decreases view of risk to investors
		"I have something, I need help scaling."
	Data to back up your idea can work, but if you don't have a solution then VC's will take more
	Market through visual representation
		Don't sell your idea, let your prototype sell it for you
