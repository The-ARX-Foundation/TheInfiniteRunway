# 1.  What did you learn about motivating employees?

Monetary compensation MUST be followed through, honestly best to not make big promises on salary unless you're really going to do it.
	"We're doing so well, looking to give out bonuses"
	Deere: ~10% of profits to bonuses every once in a while (unwritten profit share)

Defining what the new employees are to do and how that fits into the company goals is key.

# 3.  What made an employee motivation presentation compelling?
Defined what they wanted to achieve with hiring on. Mentioned motivating factors (Personal, Business Success, Customer Happines)

Have a short & catchy mission statement.

Able to be more conversational (less info on slides unless you need it)

More personal (Fiberstruct had everyone go shake someone's hand lol)
	Also didn't bring up business

Scheduling of company bonding / activities
	Tournament style bracketing of group activites was honestly kindof interesting

Community Outreach

Throwbacks to history of company

Balance of formal and informal language to keep it real lmao

Cale Solar Sat down! (less formal, on the employees level)
	Not a prescription, but lean into what works!! (I honestly really liked the sit down as an employee)

Certification Courses
	Opportunities to let people grow
	Have employees train (lets them double dip learning)

Hiring Recommendation Rewards

Inefficiency Solution Rewards
	Keep on company file and give em cash per original idea that can be implemented
	Want to make sure its not cultivating a culture of paying them extra just to do their job

Sports competition sponsoring within company

Putting faces to the new employees, defining their roles

Better set incentives
	Bonus tied to objectives




# 4.  What made an employee motivation presentation non-compelling?

"We won't be there to WATCH y'all" - employees need to know they're trusted; watch would not be the wording to use.

Stand in front of the podium!!!! (more personal)

Wordy slides

Broken Slides (If you use Canva just render them out, it won't work otherwise)

Low energy
