Course: MTDE 489 

1.  Who was your favorite guest speaker in the course and why?
My favorite guest speaker for this course would have to be Taha Raja; his insights into business development and renovation opened up doors in my mind to the concept of business renovation (may very well do so in my future).
2.  What was your favorite case study in this course and why?
Case Study 3; it provided an insight into a very common situation in the startup realm; running out of runway. I [[Hope]] to be able to bootstrap my own companies, and as such may very well run into the same problems.
3.  What was your favorite presentation you gave in this course and why?
My favorite presentation was definitely the second one, I froze on the first one and it was a way to redeem myself. Additionally, learning how to give a funding pitch is invaluable (likely one of the most applicable skills I learned from this course.)
4.  What was your favorite lecture in this course and why?
My favorite lecture would have to be the lecture on designing your organization, [[Lecture 15 - (11-05-2024)]]. I'm really into the concept of 'hyper-productivity' and automation, thus want to focus on creating processes in such a way that you can lower the skill level required to complete said tasks (and get more done in the process). 
5.  What are you taking away from this course?
Overall a new perspective on how to start my own company! I really valued having your insight throughout the semester; being able to learn from someone who's been through the thick of it and is able to clearly articulate how they got through it has been immensely beneficial.
