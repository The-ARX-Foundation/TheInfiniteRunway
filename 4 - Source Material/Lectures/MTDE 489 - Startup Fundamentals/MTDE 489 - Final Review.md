## **Final Study Outline**

### **1. Funding (Lecture 5)**

#### Key Topics:

- **Sources of Capital**:
    - Self-funding, Friends & Family, Grants, Crowdfunding, Angel Investors, VC’s.
- **Convertible Instruments**:
    - **Convertible Note**: Loan convertible into equity (interest rate, maturity date).
    - **SAFE**: Equity agreement without loans (no interest or maturity date).
    - Advantages of Convertible Instruments:
        - Quick, simple, no valuation required.
- **Series Funding (A, B, C, etc.)**:
    - Requires company valuations.
    - Involves negotiation of complex terms.

#### Study Tips:

- Understand scenarios where each funding source is ideal.
- Compare and contrast Convertible Notes and SAFEs.

---

### **2. Selling (Lecture 8)**

#### Key Topics:

- Start selling early: **Customer feedback drives development**.
- Founder-led Sales:
    - Advantages: Direct feedback, product refinement.
- **Best Practices**:
    - Sell solutions, not features.
    - Identify target market, end-users, and sales strategies (e.g., B2B, B2C, SaaS).
- Sales Channels:
    - Distributors, retailers, direct sales, leases.

#### Study Tips:

- Practice drafting a pitch focusing on the problem your business solves.
- Identify pros and cons of different sales channels.

---

### **3. Scaling (Lecture 10)**

#### Key Topics:

- Process Development:
    - Manufacturing: DFM (Design for Manufacture), QC processes.
    - Software: Development tools, coding standards, testing.
    - Services: Operational efficiency, [[Quality Control]].
- Importance of process definition for scalability.

#### Study Tips:

- Review examples of scaling strategies in manufacturing, software, and service industries.
- Understand the role of documentation in scaling.

---

### **4. Business Operations (Lecture 11)**

#### Key Topics:

- Efficient Operations:
    - Operations manual.
    - Use of efficiency tools.
    - Process dependency > person dependency.
- **Control & Monitoring**:
    - Set Goals, KPIs, and OKRs.
- Goals of Operations:
    - Increased efficiency, margins, growth, happy employees/customers.

#### Study Tips:

- Memorize how KPIs and OKRs are defined and used.
- Consider how operational goals contribute to overall strategy.

---

### **5. Organizational Design (Lecture 13)**

#### Key Topics:

- Align structure, processes, and metrics with strategy.
- **Eliminate Tribal Knowledge**:
    - Build processes driven by strategy, not individuals.
- Organizational Development:
    - Strategy assessment, defining metrics, filling roles.
- Goals:
    - Collaboration, empowerment, and strategic focus.

#### Study Tips:

- Create a mock strategy and outline how to align structure and roles.
- Think through the benefits of being process-driven.

---

### **6. Financials / GAAP (Lecture 14)**

#### Key Topics:

- **Five Account Types**:
    - Assets, Liabilities, Equity, Revenue, Expenses.
- Financial Statements:
    - **Balance Sheet**: A snapshot of Assets = Liabilities + Equity.
    - **Income Statement (P&L)**: Profit or loss over time.
    - **Cash Flow Statement**: Cash on hand and its changes.
- Depreciation vs. Amortization.
- EBITDA: Financial metric before deductions.

#### Study Tips:

- Practice interpreting financial statements.
- Understand how each account type contributes to overall financial health.

---

### **7. Valuations & Exits (Lecture 15)**

#### Key Topics:

- Valuation Methods:
    - Funding Method (early stage), Revenue Method (mid-stage), P/E Method (later stage).
- Exit Strategies:
    - IPO, Acquisition, Liquidation, Legacy, Bankruptcy.
- **Acquisition Process**:
    - Initial meetings → LOI & Negotiations → Due Diligence → Purchase Contract → Closing.

#### Study Tips:

- Compare exit strategies and their implications.
- Understand the steps in an acquisition process and their importance.
