* Set up your financial books as soon as you open a bank account for your company
	* GAAP Compliance is necessary
		* Let your accountant & bookkeepers do this
		* Be sure you can understand everything though
	* Quickbooks is dominant player
		* based on what you want your reports to tell you
		* You have to design these reports though; what accounts to charge things too

* 5 Types of accounts
	* Assets
		* resources your company own that provide value
	* Liabilities
		* debt your company owes
			* Loans
			* SAFES / Notes
	* Equity
		* Money left after subtracting liabilities from the assets
	* Revenue
		* Money that comes in
	* Expenses
		* Money that you pay out

* Income Statement
	* To be reported to SEC
	* general income and expenses
* P&L
	* essentially more detailed Income statement
	* income and expenses over a period of time
	* Over a period of time
* Balance Sheet
	* Snapshot for right now
	* Assets = Liabilities + Equity
* Cash flow statement
	* used for forecasting
	* changes in balance sheet accounts and income affect the amount of cash on hand over a period of time
