## Lecture

"Exiting starts with an on ramp"

### Tech to market model
"TRL" - Technology Readiness Level
	1. Basic principles are observed and reported
	2. Technology concept and/or application formulated
	3. Critical Function, Proof of concept established
	4. Lab testing of prototype components of process

Jolly Commercialization Model
	Goes into Proven Technology then to testing whether it is market viable
	Customer will tell you what's wrong with it
	Find Industry mentors / coaches / advisors
		Networking becomes key

## Case Study

Seacoat - made tech to make boats more efficient

Guest to Guest Lecture
	John - Founder of Seacoat

Got offered $35M for idea

Reality:
	Figure out the true value of your idea
	Subtract the work required to get it there to determine your worth

What is your motivation?
What is the buyers motivation?

#### Valuation Tools
1. Size the market
	Number of vehicles
	Percentage that could adopt solution

2. Proforma & Cash flow
	Proforma is a calculation of hella factors over five years
		Sales x sales price = projected net sales
			Attempt to grow per quarter
		Subtract COGs
		Safety Stock
		

3. Cap table
	Calculate exit table based on other factors
		Input on cap table, investment dilutions, market estimations
			Range based on all, low and high end
	Fluctuates based on percentage of market capture
		Need to ask for a copy of this spreadsheet if we can get it (even blank, seems like a cool tool)
