# Emerging Technologies:

1. Smart Eye Glasses Technology – Smart eye glasses that can be used for augmented   reality in conjunction with software.  
2. Battery Breakthrough Technology - New battery technology with 25%  more storage density, 50% faster recharge rate but cost 20% more than current technology.  
3. Remote Biometric Sensing Technology - A low cost sensing device that can monitor heart rate (pulse), blood pressure and blood oxygenation levels from a distance of 15 meters.  
4. Security Technology - A scanning device, similar to a metal detector, that spans 15 meters and uses ML/AI technology to identify specific shapes & metal objects from one of multiple bodies passing through the detector.  
5. Remote Body Temperature Scanning Technology - A scanning device, similar to a metal detector, that spans 15 meters and can accurately sense the body temperature of one out of several bodies that pass through it. 
6. Electronic Credit Card Technology - An electronic “credit card” that can have multiple credit card accounts, ID’s and access cards loaded on to it. It is slightly thicker than an actual credit card and costs about $25 to manufacture.  
7. Injectable Biometric Sensing Technology - Low cost IoT (internet of things) biosensor that can measure body temperature, blood pressure, heart rate, hydration, oxygenation, % body fat, and % muscle mass. It must be injected into the subject. Multiple sensors can report to the same cloud account.  
8. Solar Panel Breakthrough Technology - New solar panel technology that is more efficient and less costly. It can generate enough electricity to pay for its installation cost in approximately 40 months years at current  electricity rates.  
9. Advanced Building Materials - A synthetic material that has similar structural properties to wood but is 50% the cost. It can be cut with a saw, fastened with nails or screws, is lighter and non flammable.  
10. Advanced Fire & [[Heat]] Resistant Coatings - A fire and [[Heat]] resistant spray on coating. The coating can adhere to metals, plastics and wood. It takes about 24 hours to completely cure.  
11. AI/ML Imaging Technology - A low cost camera with AI/ML functions. The camera currently recognizes people, animals, and cars. It can be trained to recognize many things including other objects, colors, textures, a person/animal's movements/gestures and facial expressions. The camera has a bluetooth interface and can send out 1,000 different commands/responses.  
12. Mesh Network Agricultural Monitoring Sensing Technology - An IoT (Internet of Things) device that can measure ground moisture, salinity levels and nitrogen levels. Multiple sensors can report to the same cloud account.  
13. Advanced Material Technology - A material whose viscosity is similar to water in its natural state and changes to the viscosity of concrete in the presence of an electromagnetic field. The viscosity of the material is directly proportional to the strength of the magnetic field. Basically the material changes from a liquid to a solid when exposed to an electromagnetic field. For this to work the material must be surrounded by a coil of wire that produces the electromagnetic field. 
14. Autonomous Landscaping Technology - A lawnmower that can mow a predetermined area. It utilizes AI/ML technology to avoid gardens, fowerbeds, trees, people, children, pets, etc. The device also recognizes transitions between the lawn and other areas such as curbs and will edge the lawn along the transitions.
15. Vehicle Coupling System - This technology allows a vehicle to autonomously follow the vehicle in front of it. It is equipped with a collision avoidance system. The system can automatically couple or decouple based upon if something gets in front of the vehicle in front of it or if road conditions, such as stop signs or traffic lights are detected. The lead vehicle has a device that monitors the couple vehicles it is leading.  
16. Autonomous Drone Technology - This autonomous drone can deliver a payload that is 5cm wide, 3.5cm tall & 5cm deep. It can carry a payload that is no more than 35 lbs/15 kg in weight. The drone launches and ejects the payload at the predetermined location. The payload safely drops to the ground via a parachute. After the payload is ejected the drone travels back to its launching base.  
17. Personalized Medicine Technology - Phage technology that is used to deliver a payload (medicine, vaccine, chemotherapy, etc) to a specific type of cell in that body.  
18. Nano 3D Printer - This technology can print very small layers of conductive, non-conductive or semi-conductive material. This technology can be used to create micro-sensor or computer chips. The print speed is fairly slow and it cannot make computer chips with the  same computational density as current semiconductor processing technology.  
19. 3D Printed Bones - This technology can 3D print material that can be used to replace a broken or damaged bone in the body. The material is porous and stimulates bone and tissue growth.  
20. 4D Printer Applications - 4D printing technology are specialized materials used by a 3D printer to generate an object. The specialized material allows the 3D object to change its properties and shape when exposed to different environments, such as temperature, light, pressure, time, etc. What application can you find for this technology?
