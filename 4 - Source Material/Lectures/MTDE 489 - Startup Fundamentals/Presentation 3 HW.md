# 1.  What did you learn about making a good sales presentation?

Banter works wonders, going first helps with nerves, its hard to get numbers on fictional stuff so err off it if you can, and clean slides are a must.

# 2.  List your top 3 sales presentations.

Cale Solar
AgMesh
Solace.AI

# 3.  What made a sales presentation compelling?

Cool 3d animations, Well thought out and practiced presentations, physical demonstration

# 4.  What made a sales presentation non-compelling?

Reading off the slides, confusing cost structures, wordy slides, transition hiccups
