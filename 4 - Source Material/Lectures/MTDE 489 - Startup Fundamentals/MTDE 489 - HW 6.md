## 1.  Why is it important to spend the time to focus on defining your business operations?  What is the end goal of defining your business operations?

In order to have a successful business, you essentially have to build it in a way that it can run without you; if you want to have any time to improve the business further instead of putting out fires all day this is a bare minimum. The end goal of defining your business operations is as such; ideally in a way that the company can scale as well!
