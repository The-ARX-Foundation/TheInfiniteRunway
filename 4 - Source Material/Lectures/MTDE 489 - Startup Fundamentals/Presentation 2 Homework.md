1.  As a startup, what did you learn during the process of preparing and presenting your funding pitch?
	Practice, practice, practice!
	Its hard to have accurate predictions without data.
	Be aggressive but real (prof. deere thought 300 million was a lot for early stage)
	Though can rely on VC knowledge, should ensure anyone could understand
		Specifically healthcare regulatory pathway complications were brought up
	
2.  If you were an investor, what would be your top 3 companies you would consider investing in?
	(Shameless self promo lol) MyoSense - Implantable Myoelectric Biosensors 
	Triple S AgMesh
	Fiberstruct
	HeatShield
	
	
3.  What made a good funding pitch?
	Concise, rehearsed
	Minimal text on slides with most valuable information only
	Show ROI
	Have an MVP / Mockup!
	
	
4.  What made a not-so-good funding pitch?
	Unclear problem
	Be manipulative
	Unclear ask
