## Draft Elevator Pitch

(DAN) "Imagine being able to ==predict and prevent muscle fatigue== before it even happens, or helping a prosthetic user regain not just movement but ==true dexterity==. Today, prosthetics, sports medicine, and neuromuscular disease management all face a common challenge: the lack of precise, real-time data on muscle activity. This limitation leads to ==less effective treatments, slower recovery, and higher rates of injury==.

(LANDON) To address this challenge, our team is developing ==implantable biosensors that can detect and interpret the subtle electrical signals== from muscles with ==unmatched accuracy==. Using our ==**proprietary myoelectric sensor**== technology, we leverage ==**advanced [[Signal Processing]] and miniaturization**== to deliver ==**real-time muscle monitoring**== for actionable insights.

(DAN) Whether it’s helping athletes avoid injuries, enabling better management of neuromuscular diseases, or improving chronic pain treatment, our biosensors offer a versatile solution that can transform multiple fields. By offering unparalleled precision and real-time monitoring, we aim to drastically improve outcomes, reduce injury rates, and enhance the quality of life for many.

(LANDON) We’re eager to hear your feedback and explore how this technology can be tailored to meet the specific needs of each application. ==Together, let's revolutionize muscle monitoring and turn every flex muscle signal into a step towards better health.=="
