1.  As you startup grows and become successful, why is it important to spend time to "design" your organization?

Ideally your organization should be able to run itself. You don't want to be putting your time into putting out fires, you want to be improving the company / developing new tech instead. (My main goal is to have many autonomous organizations such that I can live my life unimpaired by heavy workloads)

2.  What is your big takeaway from guest speaker Logan Havern?  What could Mr. Havern do to improve his talk?  Would you recommend him as a speaker for this class next semester?

As someone who wants to develop SaaS products the talk with Logan Havern (Datalogz) was right up my alley, I was super excited to get the chance to talk with someone who has done this successfully; genuinely learned a lot. I do feel that he needs to have some more structure, say a visual aid of some sort (I forget if he had any visual aids other than his website, just remember that the rest of the class had difficulty engaging). I do think that he would be a good speaker to keep on for next semester as per my initial praise though.
