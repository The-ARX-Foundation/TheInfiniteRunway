Law of diminishing returns: More goals = less achieved (11-20 -> 0 achieved)
	Effort gets distributed too thin
	"Hedging Bets"

AGGIES:
	AGG - Focus on the Absolute Greatest Goal
		A goal that makes all others inconsequential if failed
			SMART != AGG, AGG == SMART
		The lower level AGG must *ensure* the success of the higher AGGs
		In the form of "From X to Y by when"
		An action is a "solution", not necessarily a goal.
		"If every other area of our operation remained the same level of performance, what is the one area where change would have the greatest impact?"
			For an organization pick an AGG that is 6 months to 2 years out
			The goal is to raise the level of performance s.t. it is a natural part of your teams workflow "reintroduce back to the whirlwind"
	Indicators - Act on Leading Indicators
		Lag measures give direct [[Statistics]] on how you are doing (in the past).
		Lead indicators are predictive and much more powerful if you can attain them.
			To determine if a good indicator, hypothesize, collect data, then test for correlations.
			"Mental model" is a good start; what improves attempts towards goal in theory?
	Expectations & Accountability - Weekly AGG meeting
		Weekly meeting lasting <30 minutes
		Review scoreboard, account for what happened last week, set expectations for next week
			"The whirlwind is not invited"
			Individual preparations:
				Review commitments
				What can I do to best improve lead indicators?
					Doesn't necessarily equate most important (i.e. whirlwind)
	Scoreboard
		Some way of displaying progress is needed
		Drives execution, need to show that the goal is actually achievable.
		"Engineering Scoreboard": Lead or lag measure on y-axis, time on x-axis

Creativity needs structure; people need to know why they do what they do.
