* Need statements for idea:
	Creatives need:
		Jobs they ==***want***== to be invested in.
		An easier way to be discovered by clients.
			An algorithm that works for them, rather than an algorithm that they work for.
				To be more than just content creators.
		A way to run their business in the background
			"Having to run my hobby as a business to justify spending time on it has drained the  [[Passion]] from my work"
			Gamification?
			Unity of all business tools so they can stay on one platform
			Business management tools / resources
				potentially even connections to managers themselves
		Direction to resources to learn skills that people want them to have
			Easier [[Market Research]]
			Ways to determine how to keep one's style without closing too many doors
			
	
	Clients Need:
		An easy way to hire a creative solution
			Sometimes to find a problem in the first place!
				EX. Low level shifting factors to be triggered by discovery event
		Engaged creatives
			Often the master of one over the jack of all trades
				Specific aesthetic
				Specific workflow
				Specific _
		One platform for all of their needs (local or remote)
			EX. Content creator might need graphic designer ([[Thumbnails]] and marketing), video editor, captioning, [[Social Media]] management, advertising specialists, etc.
			Ex. Wedding planner might need photographer (local), videographer (local), designer for cards etc. (remote)
