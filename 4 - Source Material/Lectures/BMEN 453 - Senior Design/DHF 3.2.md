Refined Problem Statement:
Medtronic's existing documentation management system is facing significant challenges in coping with the rapidly growing complexity and volume of engineering documents. As the company continues to make new products, specifically through acquisitions and expansions, these documents multiply and diversify. One issue is difficulty accessing information. Engineers are struggling to quickly locate and retrieve relevant documents. This inefficiency is particularly problematic when dealing with time-sensitive projects that require rapid iteration and regulatory submission. Another issue is regulatory compliance. As documentation grows in volume, ensuring that all necessary forms, patents, and regulatory information are readily accessible becomes more challenging. The inability to efficiently manage and retrieve these documents raises the risk of non-compliance. A third issue is the integration of documentation from acquired companies, which has introduced additional complexity. Merging these documents into Medtronic's existing systems without compromising accessibility or introducing errors is difficult. Therefore, there is a need for a new documentation management solution that can enhance searchability and accessibility. This solution should be capable of handling the growing volume and complexity of documents so that all teams can efficiently access the information they need.
