### **Product/Procedure/Service 1: Microsoft CoPilot Notebook [1]**

#### **Activities**
- **Document Formulation:** Users generate, edit, and refine documents, leveraging AI to assist in content creation, grammar checking, and contextual suggestions.
- **Project Management:** Users utilize AI-driven tools to manage tasks, timelines, and workflows within project documentation.
- **Learning and Research:** Students and professionals use the platform to gather information, take notes, and structure research documents.
  
#### **Environments**
- **Digital Workspaces:** Primarily accessed through a web browser or a dedicated desktop application, users interact within an environment optimized for productivity, collaboration, and integration with Microsoft Office Suite.
- **Remote and Hybrid Work Settings:** The tool is utilized in environments where users may be working remotely or in hybrid setups, necessitating seamless cloud access and collaboration features.

#### **Interactions**
- **AI Assistance:** Users interact with AI to receive real-time suggestions, summaries, and corrections within their documents.
- **Collaboration Tools:** Integrated with Microsoft's collaboration features, users can share, comment on, and co-edit documents with others.
- **Task Automation:** AI automates routine tasks such as formatting, generating reports, and managing document versions.

#### **Objects**
- **Documents:** Word documents, spreadsheets, presentations, and other project-related files that users create and manage.
- **Cloud Storage:** Integrated with OneDrive and SharePoint for seamless access and backup.
- **AI-Enhanced Tools:** Features like real-time grammar checking, content suggestions, and project management templates.

#### **Users**
- **Students:** Utilizing the tool for note-taking, assignment creation, and research documentation.
- **Engineers:** Leveraging AI to maintain technical documentation, manage project workflows, and ensure consistency in reporting.
- **Writers:** Using the AI to draft, edit, and polish written content across various formats.

### **Product/Procedure/Service 2: ChatGPT (Base) [2]**

#### **Activities**
- **General LLM Chat:** Engaging in natural language conversations for a wide range of topics, from casual inquiries to detailed research discussions.
- **Educational Support:** Providing explanations, tutoring, and guidance across multiple subjects and educational levels.
- **Content Generation:** Users employ the AI to create written content, brainstorm ideas, or provide feedback on drafts.

#### **Environments**
- **Web Browsers:** Primarily accessed through browsers, the interface is designed for quick and easy interactions.
- **Digital Learning Environments:** Used in online courses, e-learning platforms, and remote education settings.

#### **Interactions**
- **Conversational AI:** Users input questions or prompts and receive AI-generated responses, mimicking a human-like dialogue.
- **Document Upload and Analysis:** Users can upload documents for the AI to analyze, summarize, or provide feedback on.
- **Exploratory Learning:** Users engage in a back-and-forth dialogue with the AI to explore topics in depth, akin to a digital tutor.

#### **Objects**
- **Chat Interface:** The primary mode of interaction, where users input text and receive AI responses.
- **Uploaded Documents:** Files that users submit for analysis or content generation.
- **Generated Content:** Outputs such as text summaries, answers, and content drafts.

#### **Users**
- **Everyone:** The tool is versatile and can be used by a broad audience, including students, engineers, writers, and general users seeking information or content generation.

### **Product/Procedure/Service 3: ChatGPT (API) [3]**

#### **Activities**
- **Smart Feature Implementation:** Developers integrate AI capabilities into their applications, enabling features like natural language processing, automated responses, and intelligent search.
- **Task Automation:** The API is used to automate menial tasks, reducing the need for manual intervention in repetitive processes.
- **Custom AI Development:** Engineers and developers build custom solutions that leverage the API to create specialized applications, such as smart documentation systems or AI-driven customer support.

#### **Environments**
- **Integrated Development Environments (IDEs):** The API is used within IDEs where developers write and test code, ensuring seamless integration with existing software.
- **Backend Systems:** Deployed in server environments where the AI processes user input and generates responses, often as part of a larger application.

#### **Interactions**
- **API Calls:** Developers interact with the API through code, sending requests and receiving responses that can be integrated into their applications.
- **NLP Tokenization:** The API processes natural language input on the backend, converting it into structured data for further processing.
- **Automated Feedback:** The API provides real-time suggestions, completions, or content generation based on the input it receives.

#### **Objects**
- **API Interface:** The endpoint through which developers interact with ChatGPT’s capabilities.
- **Development Tools:** Software such as IDEs, version control systems, and debugging tools that support the integration process.
- **Generated Code/Content:** Outputs produced by the API, such as code snippets, documentation, or automated responses.

#### **Users**
- **Software Developers:** Primary users who integrate the API into applications, enhancing their software with AI-driven features.
- **Researchers:** Utilizing the API to create intelligent systems for data analysis, automation, or other specialized tasks.

### **Product/Procedure/Service 4: ChatGPT (Custom GPT) [4]**

#### **Activities**
- **LLM Chat Specialization:** Developing and deploying specialized language models tailored to specific domains, such as medical research, legal documentation, or technical support.
- **Custom Knowledge Base Configuration:** Users configure the GPT model with a specific knowledge base, ensuring it can provide expert-level responses in its specialized area.
- **Advanced Education and Training:** Leveraging the custom GPT for specialized training programs, providing targeted learning experiences in fields like engineering, medicine, or law.

#### **Environments**
- **Web-Based Applications:** Accessed through browsers or dedicated applications, where users can interact with the specialized GPT model.
- **Field-Specific Workspaces:** Deployed in environments where specialized knowledge is crucial, such as corporate training platforms, research institutions, or industry-specific support systems.

#### **Interactions**
- **Custom AI Interface:** Users interact with the specialized GPT through a tailored chat interface designed to address specific needs and contexts.
- **Document Upload for Specialized Analysis:** Users can upload field-specific documents, which the AI analyzes and provides insights or summaries tailored to that domain.
- **Personalized AI Responses:** The GPT model provides highly specialized answers, advice, or content generation based on the configured knowledge base.

#### **Objects**
- **Specialized Knowledge Base:** A collection of field-specific data and documents that the GPT model is trained on or configured to reference.
- **Custom Chat Interface:** The user-facing component where specialized interactions take place, designed to reflect the unique requirements of the field.
- **Field-Specific Documentation:** Documents that users upload for analysis, training, or content generation.

#### **Users**
- **Specialized Use Cases:** Users from specific fields, such as medical researchers, legal professionals, or engineers, who require expert-level interactions and content generation.
- **Software Developers:** Those who configure and deploy the custom GPT for specific applications or industries.
- **Students and Professionals:** Individuals engaged in specialized training or research, benefiting from the targeted expertise provided by the custom GPT.
