## Jose Escobedo, R&D Engineer

Stakeholder 1: Research & Development – this department is in charge of researching and developing new devices/therapies for medical device companies like Medtronic. They would interact with potential solutions when they look for relevant SOPs and other work instructions to perform experiments. They also keep track of everything they do in the lab, which will eventually be uploaded to the solution.

---

### Bio:

Jose Escobedo is a Senior Mechanical Engineer with extensive experience in the medical device field. His career includes nearly three years at Caldera Medical, where he worked on developing mechanical components for medical devices, and now at Medtronic, where he plays a key role in the [[Mechanical Engineering]] aspects of new product development. A former NIH BUILD scholar, Jose also has a background in biomedical research, with interests in automation, novel materials, and robotics.

Key Takeaways for the Team:

- Jose’s engineering background and hands-on approach make him a valuable source of insights into the practical challenges engineers face when managing documentation.
    
- His experience spans both the development and testing of mechanical components, meaning he regularly works with technical specs, material data, and compliance documentation.
    
- His research interests in automation and novel materials align well with potential solutions for streamlining documentation processes in the future.
    

---

### Additional Notes:

- Focus on Practicality: Since Jose is more hands-on, focus on how documentation tools impact the practical aspects of his work, such as accessing the right specs quickly, ensuring data accuracy, and managing version control.

### 6. Vision for the Future of Documentation
