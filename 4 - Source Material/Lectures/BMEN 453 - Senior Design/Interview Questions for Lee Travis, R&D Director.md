## **Lee Travis, R\&D Director**

**Stakeholder Type 1:** *Research & Development* – this department is in charge of researching and developing new devices/therapies for medical device companies like Medtronic. They would interact with potential solutions when they look for relevant SOPs and other work instructions to perform experiments. They also keep track of everything they do in the lab, which will eventually be uploaded to the solution.

---

### *Bio:*

Lee Travis is a seasoned engineering leader with over 28 years of experience in medical device development. Throughout his career, Lee has built and led high-performing technical teams, fostering environments of accountability, collaboration, and [[Trust]]. Known for his hands-on leadership style, he excels at managing teams through all phases of the medical device product lifecycle, including requirements, risk management, design, engineering, verification, validation, and manufacturing transfer.

**Key Takeaways for the Team:**

* Lee has decades of hands-on experience leading medical device teams through all phases of product development, from early R\&D to post-launch support.  
* He has deep insights into the documentation and regulatory needs that are critical for ensuring product compliance and successful market entry.  
* Lee’s leadership [[Philosophy]] focuses on building strong, collaborative teams, making him an ideal stakeholder to discuss challenges and opportunities in improving documentation workflows.  
* His extensive experience across multiple companies, both large and small, offers valuable perspectives on integrating systems, managing complex projects, and addressing technical challenges.

### *Introduction:*

#### **Background and Role:**

1. Could you start by introducing yourself and sharing a bit about your time at Medtronic, especially your role within the Diabetes New Ventures team?  
2. How does your work here compare to your previous roles in R\&D leadership across other medical device companies?

### *General Documentation Workflow:*

**Current Process Overview:**

3. Can you describe the typical process your team follows when accessing or managing critical documentation for a new product development project?  
4. How do teams generally retrieve SOPs, technical specs, or regulatory requirements?

**System Design Aspirations:**

5. If you were to design the ideal documentation system to support your team, what core functionalities would it absolutely need?  
6. How would it integrate with other departments like manufacturing or regulatory?

### *Challenges in Information Access:*

**Obstacles in Documentation Management:**

7. From your perspective as an engineering leader, what are the biggest challenges your teams face when locating important documentation?  
8. Are there any recurring issues that have significantly impacted R\&D timelines or efficiency?

**Real-World Example of Challenges:**

9. Can you think of a recent project where difficulty in finding the right documentation led to delays or other issues?  
10. How did that affect the project, and how was the situation resolved?

### *Desired Improvements and Ideal Solutions:*

**Key Problem to Solve:**

11. If you could address just one critical issue with how documentation is accessed or organized today, what would that be, especially considering your leadership role across multiple technical teams?

**Envisioning an Ideal Solution:**

12. If a new tool could make document access significantly faster and more intuitive, how would that look in your team’s day-to-day operations?  
13. How would you expect it to interface with other tools like risk management or validation systems?

**Automation of Repetitive Tasks:**

14. Are there any documentation-related processes that your teams repeatedly struggle with?  
15. How do you think automation or intelligent systems could streamline these tasks and reduce the administrative burden?

### *Collaboration and Communication Needs:*
