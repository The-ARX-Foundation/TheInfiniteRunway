* “You can start now.” 
	* Why not? Find something you [[Love]] and push for it.

* [[Social Media]] can fuel your learning process, post while you go, get a following!
	* Can use platform to run diagnostic / get feedback.
		* Playtesting

* Make sure you think about the liability aspects
	* People can find your platform and try to connect with you.
		* “Don’t post content about your business, post content about your founder.”
		* Use TikTok for the people, use Instagram and Facebook for ads.

* User demographics are different.

* Find an imbalance and try to solve it.
	* Gender disparity within golf clubs was her imbalance.

* Innovation can occur in fitting systems for product personalization.
	* Why can’t you ship all the options? Make something adjustable.
	* Create some user centered diagnostics to let the user find what works best for them.
		* Distance of put, direction of put, weight preferences mentioned.

* Machining at rellis campus.
	* Contact manufacturing majors.
	* University profs might know people who do whatever kind of manufacturing that you may need.

* Get provisional patent ASAP.

* Look into competitions for non-diluted funds.
	* Can lead to networking cascades if idea is good enough.
	* ALWAYS PRACTICE YOUR PITCHES
		* “Home in on your idea, home in on your pitch”
		* Practice as much as you can.
		* You need to show how much you [[Love]] your idea.
		* Think about pitch coaches “asking for advice is okay”.
		* You need to make sure everyone can understand your product, not everyone is an engineer.

* They need to walk away knowing what they need to know.

* You can loan your business money if it is an LLC, keep spreadsheet of expenses.

* Team Comp:
	* Find someone that thinks differently than you.
