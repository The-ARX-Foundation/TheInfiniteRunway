* Worked consulting for entire working life.
	* Challenge that is project based and you wouldn’t get bored with.
	* Training and learning for life.
	* Opportunities to work with so many great people.

* “If you think about consulting in general there are many types:”
	* Hypothesis / Strategy Consultation
		* What do they need to do?
	* Operational Consultation
		* How can they make it happen?
	* Technological Consultation
	* Niche Consultation
		* Seems the most my speed, I like actual product development.
	* “You can be successful in any of these as long as you know who you are.”

* “Work ethic and attitude is still a big deal.”
	* Aggies are known for having it, so you better have it.

* “Live every day as if it is your last.”
	* Even though it is hard, take it in, live in it.

* Change management in organizations:
	* Alignment of plan, strategy, and goals of stakeholders is necessary for success.

Questions:
* How did you get started?
	* Found a company that would train him for consulting.
		* Taught him the industries, business processes, etc. to get up to speed.
		* Learned how to “keep the end goal in mind, how to create the steps to get there”.
	* “Resist the temptation to be an expert on day one, explore your field.”
		* “Will make you a better consultant at the end of the day.”
		* “You will learn more about what you are more passionate about.”

* How do you get your skills out there?
	* Internal [[Resumes]] (skill oriented) back in the day, now LinkedIn
		* Make sure your stories are more than present, “the experiences are just as important, good or bad”.

* How do you manage family life?
	* Your partner must support you and be willing to sacrifice having you not around sometimes.
	* “If you want to make it work, you make it work.”
	* Covid has people re-thinking travel of consultants (can do remote options).

* Can I start my own consulting firm?
	* “Great because you get to do everything, but its really important and a challenge to keep busy.”
	* “Work at a big firm for a couple of years to learn the delivery side etc. but then find some people you know you work really well with then make your own firm.”

* What wasn’t present in your engineering degree that is filled by experience?
	* Mainly the people skills / “soft skills”
	* Basic listening / communication skills

* Internships / jobs?
	* Variety, change industries as much as possible.
	* “Don’t be afraid to get into the guts.”
		* You may hate it but it will teach you the skills you need.
	* If you can, find some jobs that will let you sub in for people in actual jobs; shows you a variety of activities / positions in the company.

* Financial skills?
	* Accounting courses, over time take advantage of any company training.
		* Look at the people you help along with your own as a company.

* Jobs gone wrong?
	* “Get to it early.”
		* Don’t lead them on, change your plan if needed.
	* “Let them know you are there to figure things out; you will always get through it if you are up front with things.”
		* “Open dialogue”

* Work split within team?
	* Early on do client facing time with team and individuals.
	* Split as time goes on per specificities.
	* At end do more client facing time for training / getting them converted to changes.

* IP?
	* Big issue, once again “get on it early.”

* How to be best prepared for a management consulting role?
	* The biggest thing is the willingness to continue to learn.
	* Have some stories in your life about things you’ve learned, challenges you have faced.
