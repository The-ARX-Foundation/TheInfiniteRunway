## Plant Care Script:
### Intro with personal connection:
My wife really likes house plants, I mean she LOVES plants... But that doesn't necessarily mean we can keep them alive :/
### Expansion of problem to retail:
Luckily, even though we're tight on cash, we often find that there is no shortage of sickly plants on clearance that we can try to resuscitate. 

But that got me thinking, why are there so many plants on clearance? Surely these big retailers would've optimized plant care by now, but given our dilemma I don't believe this is the case!
### Retail Cash Monies:
Upon some digging, I found that an astonishing 40% of plants perish in the supply chain.

With the global indoor plant market was valued at 20 billion dollars in 2023, let alone the outdoor plant market at over 100 billion, the revenue loss is immense.

In this case, while money does grow on trees, it dies with said trees. 
### Consumer Cash Monies:
On the consumer side, I found that on average over a third of the remaining plants that do end up getting to customers die within a month of purchase. 

Daily care is often needed, and as the cost of replacing a plant ranges anywhere from $10 - $200 per plant depending on species and stage of growth, the stakes can be high.

ON TOP OF THAT higher cost plants are typically harder to care for, so why would anyone buy them?
### Solution Soft-launch:
By improving plant longevity, we can directly improve sales and revenue for plant shops and general [[Satisfaction]] for plant parents worldwide.

Whether it be quick and easy access to relevant information for each plant, scheduling help and reminders, or even automation to circumvent [[The Human]] aspect entirely; there is a significant need for change in the way we care for our plant pals. 



## Needs
### Suppliers Need:
A way to extend the lifetime of their stock internally
A way to guarantee plant health to retailers prior to sale
### Retailers Need:
A way to ensure they're buying a healthy plant
	Proof that it has been properly taken care of
A way to guarantee plant health to consumers prior to sale
A way to extend the lifetime of their stock internally
A way to differentiate themselves from the competition
	Potentially something to provide ***with*** their product
### Consumers Need:
A way to ensure they're buying a healthy plant
	Proof that it has been properly taken care of
A way to increase the length of time they get to spend with their investments (plants)
A way to justify purchasing more expensive (harder to care for) plants
