**Overview**  
This analysis discusses the market size estimation process for the hypothetical startup, Good Neighbors, focusing on borrowing tools among suburban homeowners. The market sizing is approached through a bottom-up analysis, which offers a more realistic view of potential customer engagement and revenue generation.

### Key Concepts

1. **Market Segmentation**
    
    - The target market is defined as suburban homeowners needing tools (e.g., power washers).
    - Segmentation helps narrow down the audience and focus the analysis.
2. **Market Size Estimates**
    
    - The analysis employs the TAM (Total Addressable Market), SAM (Serviceable Available Market), and SOM (Serviceable Obtainable Market) framework, albeit without explicitly labeling them.

### Market Size Breakdown

#### 1. **Total Addressable Market (TAM)**

- **Calculation of Homes**:
    - Total detached homes in the U.S. = 64 million (Urban Institute).
    - Percentage of suburban homes = 52% (Office of Policy Development & Research).
    - Neighborly behavior (those willing to lend/borrow) = 53% (Neighborhoods.com).
    - **Formula**: TAM=64M×52%×53%=17.5M homes\text{TAM} = 64M \times 52\% \times 53\% = 17.5M \text{ homes}TAM=64M×52%×53%=17.5M homes

#### 2. **Serviceable Available Market (SAM)**

- **Targeting Mid-sized Cities**:
    - Number of mid-sized cities (population < 500,000) = 441 (Metro Ideas Project).
    - Mid-sized cities represent 20% of the U.S. population.
    - **Formula**: SAM=17.5M×20%=3.5M homes\text{SAM} = 17.5M \times 20\% = 3.5M \text{ homes}SAM=17.5M×20%=3.5M homes

#### 3. **Serviceable Obtainable Market (SOM)**

- **User Base Projection**:
    - Estimated market share = 10% of SAM.
    - Expected active users = 350,000.
    - Revenue model: $10/month/user.
    - **Formula**: SOM=350,000×$10×12=$42M annually\text{SOM} = 350,000 \times \$10 \times 12 = \$42M \text{ annually}SOM=350,000×$10×12=$42M annually

#### 4. **Existing Tools Rental Market (Contextual Data)**

- **Residential Tools Rental Market**:
    - Total small tools rental market = $3.7B (The Motley Fool).
    - Percentage of residential rentals = 37% (Grand View Research).
    - **Calculation**: $3.7B×37%=$355M residential tools rental market\$3.7B \times 37\% = \$355M \text{ residential tools rental market}$3.7B×37%=$355M residential tools rental market

### Additional Insights

- The **growth potential** of the Good Neighbors platform is illustrated by juxtaposing the projected SOM ($42M) against the existing $355M tools rental market.
- The analysis acknowledges the **limitations** of using a top-down approach. Instead, the focus is on customer willingness and behavior, reinforcing the bottom-up estimation.

### Conclusion

The market size analysis for Good Neighbors demonstrates a thorough understanding of the target demographic and strategic estimation processes. While the calculations could be refined, they offer a **solid foundation** for storytelling in pitch decks, showcasing growth potential and market dynamics. The emphasis on neighborly interactions also aligns with evolving social trends towards community sharing and sustainability.
