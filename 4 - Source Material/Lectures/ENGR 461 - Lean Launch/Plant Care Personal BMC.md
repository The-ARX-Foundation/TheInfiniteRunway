### 1. **Customer Segments**

- **Suppliers**: Wholesale plant nurseries needing to extend the lifetime of their stock and provide healthy plants to retailers.
- **Retailers**: Stores (big box and plant-specific) needing to ensure they sell healthy plants and maintain stock health.
- **Consumers**: Plant enthusiasts and general consumers, especially those investing in higher-cost plants, who need help keeping their plants alive.

### 2. **Problem**

- 40% of plants perish in the supply chain.
- Over a third of plants sold to consumers die within a month.
- High mortality leads to lost revenue for both retailers and suppliers, and dissatisfaction for consumers.

### 3. **Unique Value Proposition**

- For **suppliers and retailers**: A solution that guarantees plant health and extends stock longevity, reducing mortality and increasing revenue.
- For **consumers**: A plant care tool that provides easy access to plant information, care scheduling, and automation to help plants thrive, making higher-cost plants more attractive.

### 4. **Solution**

- Create a system for suppliers and retailers to monitor and improve plant health (e.g., IoT sensors, plant care protocols).
- Develop a consumer app providing reminders, care guides, and possibly automation integration (smart watering systems).

### 5. **Channels**

- **Suppliers and Retailers**: B2B sales, partnerships with plant wholesalers, and retail chains.
- **Consumers**: Direct marketing via plant stores, plant-care influencers, and online plant communities.

### 6. **Revenue Streams**

- Subscription or licensing model for suppliers and retailers using plant health monitoring tools.
- Freemium model for consumers, with paid features for advanced care tools, reminders, or integration with smart plant care devices.

### 7. **Cost Structure**

- Development costs for plant monitoring systems and app.
- Marketing and sales costs, particularly B2B outreach.
- Operating costs: servers for app hosting, maintenance of the system.
- R&D for integrating smart devices with the app for automated plant care.

### 8. **Key Activities**

- Development of monitoring and tracking tools for plant health.
- Building partnerships with suppliers and retailers.
- Marketing to plant enthusiasts and consumers interested in smart care solutions.

### 9. **Key Resources**

- Plant care experts for developing protocols and content.
- Development team for building app and IoT integration.
- Partnerships with suppliers and retailers to pilot plant health solutions.

### 10. **Key Partners**

- Plant wholesalers and nurseries.
- Retailers looking to improve their stock turnover and customer [[Satisfaction]].
- IoT hardware providers for smart watering systems or sensors.

### 11. **Unfair Advantage**

- Integration of IoT smart systems for automated plant care.
- Deep partnerships with major plant suppliers and retailers, providing industry-specific insights and solutions.
- Network effect: As more consumers adopt the app, more data improves care advice for other users.
