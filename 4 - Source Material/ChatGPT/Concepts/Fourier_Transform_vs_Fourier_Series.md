# Fourier Transform vs Fourier Series

## Definition
-
- The Fourier Transform and Fourier Series are both methods to express functions as sums of sinusoids, but the Fourier Transform is for continuous functions over an infinite domain, while the Fourier Series is for periodic functions over a finite interval.
